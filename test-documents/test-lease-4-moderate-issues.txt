COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Commercial Lease Agreement ("Lease") is entered into on May 1, 2025, between Downtown Realty Group ("Landlord") and Creative Studio Inc. ("Tenant") for the premises located at 321 Art District Way, Sarasota, Sarasota County, Florida 34236.

TERM AND RENT

Section 1.1: The initial term of this Lease shall be two (2) years, commencing on June 1, 2025, and ending on May 31, 2027. The monthly rent shall be $5,500.00, due on the first day of each month.

Section 1.2: Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $11,000.00 upon execution of this Lease. The security deposit shall be held by Landlord and may be applied to damages or unpaid rent.

LANDLORD OBLIGATIONS

Section 2.1: Landlord shall maintain the building structure and common areas. Landlord shall provide heating and cooling systems.

Section 2.2: Landlord may enter the premises for repairs with reasonable notice.

TENANT OBLIGATIONS

Section 3.1: Tenant shall use the premises for commercial art studio purposes only. Tenant shall maintain the premises in good condition.

Section 3.2: Tenant shall not make alterations without Landlord's written consent.

Section 3.3: Tenant shall be responsible for all utilities.

DEFAULT AND TERMINATION

Section 4.1: If Tenant fails to pay rent when due, Landlord may terminate this Lease upon five (5) days written notice. Tenant shall be liable for unpaid rent and damages.

Section 4.2: If Tenant abandons the premises, Landlord may re-enter after providing notice.

LIABILITY AND INSURANCE

Section 5.1: Tenant shall maintain liability insurance with coverage of $500,000.00. Tenant shall indemnify Landlord from claims arising from Tenant's use.

Section 5.2: Landlord is not responsible for damage to Tenant's property.

ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

Section 6.1: Tenant may not assign or sublet without Landlord's consent.

GOVERNING LAW

Section 7.1: This Lease shall be governed by Florida law.
