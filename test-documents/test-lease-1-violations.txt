COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Commercial Lease Agreement ("Lease") is entered into on January 15, 2025, between ABC Properties LLC ("Landlord") and XYZ Retail Inc. ("Tenant") for the premises located at 456 Business Blvd, Tampa, Hillsborough County, Florida 33601.

TERM AND RENT

Section 1.1: The initial term of this Lease shall be two (2) years, commencing on February 1, 2025, and ending on January 31, 2027. The monthly rent shall be $4,500.00, due on the first day of each month.

Section 1.2: Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $9,000.00 upon execution of this Lease. The security deposit shall be held by Landlord and may be applied to any damages, unpaid rent, or other charges owed by Tenant. Landlord may use the security deposit for any purpose.

LANDLORD OBLIGATIONS

Section 2.1: Landlord shall maintain the building structure. Landlord is not responsible for repairs to common areas.

Section 2.2: Landlord shall provide basic utilities. Tenant is responsible for all other costs.

TENANT OBLIGATIONS

Section 3.1: Tenant shall use the premises for retail purposes only. Tenant shall not make any alterations without Landlord's consent.

Section 3.2: Tenant shall maintain the premises in good condition.

DEFAULT AND TERMINATION

Section 4.1: If Tenant fails to pay rent when due, Landlord may terminate this Lease upon three (3) days written notice. Tenant shall be liable for all unpaid rent, damages, and attorney's fees.

Section 4.2: If Tenant abandons the premises, Landlord may immediately re-enter and take possession without notice.

LIABILITY AND INSURANCE

Section 5.1: Tenant shall maintain liability insurance. Tenant shall indemnify and hold Landlord harmless from any claims.

Section 5.2: Landlord shall not be liable for any damage to Tenant's property, regardless of cause.

ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

Section 6.1: Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet any portion without Landlord's consent, which may be withheld for any reason.

GOVERNING LAW

Section 7.1: This Lease shall be governed by the laws of the State of Florida.
