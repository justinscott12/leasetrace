COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Commercial Lease Agreement ("Lease") is entered into on March 1, 2025, between Sunshine Properties Inc. ("Landlord") and Tech Solutions LLC ("Tenant") for the premises located at 789 Innovation Drive, St. Petersburg, Pinellas County, Florida 33701.

TERM AND RENT

Section 1.1: The initial term of this Lease shall be three (3) years, commencing on April 1, 2025, and ending on March 31, 2028. The monthly rent shall be $6,000.00, due on the first day of each month.

Section 1.2: Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $12,000.00 upon execution of this Lease. The security deposit shall be held by Landlord in a separate interest-bearing account as required by Florida law. The security deposit may be applied to any damages, unpaid rent, or other charges owed by Tenant, subject to the requirements of Florida Statute § 83.49.

LANDLORD OBLIGATIONS

Section 2.1: Landlord shall maintain the structural integrity of the building, including the roof, foundation, and exterior walls, in compliance with Florida Statute § 83.51. Landlord shall be responsible for repairs to common areas and building systems.

Section 2.2: Landlord shall provide heating and air conditioning systems in good working order. Landlord shall maintain all building systems in compliance with applicable building codes.

Section 2.3: Landlord shall provide at least seven (7) days written notice before entering the premises for non-emergency repairs, in compliance with Florida Statute § 83.53.

TENANT OBLIGATIONS

Section 3.1: Tenant shall use the premises solely for office purposes and shall not use the premises for any illegal activities or purposes that violate local zoning ordinances.

Section 3.2: Tenant shall maintain the premises in good condition and shall not make any alterations or improvements without Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

Section 3.3: Tenant shall be responsible for all utilities, including electricity, water, sewer, and trash removal, unless otherwise specified.

DEFAULT AND TERMINATION

Section 4.1: If Tenant fails to pay rent when due, Landlord may terminate this Lease upon seven (7) days written notice as required by Florida Statute § 83.56. Tenant shall be liable for all unpaid rent, damages, and reasonable attorney's fees.

Section 4.2: If Tenant abandons the premises or fails to occupy the premises for more than thirty (30) consecutive days, Landlord may re-enter and take possession of the premises after providing proper notice as required by law.

LIABILITY AND INSURANCE

Section 5.1: Tenant shall maintain comprehensive general liability insurance with minimum coverage of $1,000,000.00. Tenant shall provide proof of insurance to Landlord upon request. Tenant shall indemnify and hold Landlord harmless from any claims arising from Tenant's use of the premises, except to the extent caused by Landlord's negligence.

Section 5.2: Landlord shall maintain property insurance for the building structure. Landlord shall not be liable for damage to Tenant's personal property except to the extent caused by Landlord's negligence or failure to maintain the premises as required by law.

ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

Section 6.1: Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet any portion of the premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld. Landlord may condition consent on the assignee or subtenant meeting reasonable financial and business criteria.

HOLDOVER

Section 7.1: If Tenant remains in possession after the expiration of the Lease term without Landlord's written consent, Tenant shall be deemed a holdover tenant and shall pay rent at 150% of the monthly rent amount, subject to applicable law.

ENTIRE AGREEMENT

Section 8.1: This Lease constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, or agreements. This Lease may only be modified by a written instrument signed by both parties.

GOVERNING LAW

Section 9.1: This Lease shall be governed by the laws of the State of Florida. Any disputes arising under this Lease shall be resolved in the courts of Pinellas County, Florida, in accordance with Florida Statute Chapter 83.

SECURITY DEPOSIT RETURN

Section 10.1: Upon termination of this Lease, Landlord shall return the security deposit to Tenant within fifteen (15) days, less any deductions for damages, unpaid rent, or other charges, as required by Florida Statute § 83.49. Landlord shall provide an itemized list of any deductions.
