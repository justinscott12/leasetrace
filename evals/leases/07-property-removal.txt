Owner: Mangrove Point Residential LLC
Resident(s): Alicia Brandt and Thomas Brandt
Dwelling: Apartment 3307, The Reserve at Mangrove Point, 8800 Mangrove Point Parkway, Naples, Florida 34108. The Reserve is a 312-unit apartment community.
Term: 12 months beginning March 1, 2027 and ending February 29, 2028.
Rent: $2,275 monthly.

SECTION 1. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Residents have paid a $1,000 security deposit. Owner holds the deposit in a separate non-interest-bearing account at Collier Coast Bank, 1500 Fifth Avenue South, Naples, Florida 34102, and Residents are not entitled to interest. If Owner makes no claim, the deposit will be refunded within 15 days after move-out; notice of any claim will be sent by certified mail within 30 days after move-out.

YOUR RENTAL AGREEMENT REQUIRES PAYMENT OF CERTAIN DEPOSITS. THE LANDLORD MAY TRANSFER ADVANCE RENTS TO THE LANDLORD'S ACCOUNT AS THEY ARE DUE AND WITHOUT NOTICE. WHEN YOU MOVE OUT, YOU MUST GIVE THE LANDLORD YOUR NEW ADDRESS SO THAT THE LANDLORD CAN SEND YOU NOTICES REGARDING YOUR DEPOSIT. THE LANDLORD MUST PROVIDE YOU WRITTEN NOTICE IN PERSON, BY MAIL, OR BY E-MAIL IN ACCORDANCE WITH SECTION 83.505, FLORIDA STATUTES, WITHIN 30 DAYS AFTER YOU MOVE OUT, OF THE LANDLORD'S INTENT TO IMPOSE A CLAIM AGAINST THE DEPOSIT. IF YOU DO NOT REPLY TO THE LANDLORD STATING YOUR OBJECTION TO THE CLAIM WITHIN 15 DAYS AFTER RECEIPT OF THE LANDLORD'S WRITTEN NOTICE, THE LANDLORD WILL COLLECT THE CLAIM AND MUST MAIL YOU THE REMAINING DEPOSIT, IF ANY. IF THE LANDLORD FAILS TO TIMELY PROVIDE YOU NOTICE, THE LANDLORD MUST RETURN THE DEPOSIT BUT MAY LATER FILE A LAWSUIT AGAINST YOU FOR DAMAGES. IF YOU FAIL TO TIMELY OBJECT TO A CLAIM, THE LANDLORD MAY COLLECT FROM THE DEPOSIT, BUT YOU MAY LATER FILE A LAWSUIT CLAIMING A REFUND. YOU SHOULD ATTEMPT TO INFORMALLY RESOLVE ANY DISPUTE BEFORE FILING A LAWSUIT. GENERALLY, THE PARTY IN WHOSE FAVOR A JUDGMENT IS RENDERED WILL BE AWARDED COSTS AND ATTORNEY FEES PAYABLE BY THE LOSING PARTY. THIS DISCLOSURE IS BASIC. PLEASE REFER TO PART II OF CHAPTER 83, FLORIDA STATUTES, TO DETERMINE YOUR LEGAL RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS.

SECTION 2. LATE PAYMENT
Rent paid after the 3rd is late. A late fee of 10% of the monthly rent applies.

SECTION 3. NONPAYMENT
If rent is not paid when due, Owner may deliver a written notice giving Residents 3 days, excluding Saturday, Sunday, and legal holidays, to pay or vacate.

SECTION 4. REMOVAL OF PROPERTY FOR NONPAYMENT
If rent is more than five days late, Owner may enter the Dwelling and remove Residents' personal property to a storage facility until all past-due rent is paid.

SECTION 5. PROPERTY LEFT BEHIND
After Residents move out, Owner is not responsible for storing or disposing of any personal property Residents leave in the Dwelling.

SECTION 6. UTILITY WORK
Owner may temporarily interrupt water or electricity to the Dwelling when necessary to make repairs to the building.

SECTION 7. EVENTS BEYOND CONTROL
If fire, hurricane, flood, war, epidemic, or other events beyond Owner's control interfere with Owner's performance, Owner is excused from further performance of its obligations under this Lease Contract for the duration of the event.

SECTION 8. ENTRY
Owner may enter the Dwelling for repairs between 8:00 a.m. and 7:30 p.m. after giving at least 24 hours' notice.

SECTION 9. MOVE-OUT NOTICE
Residents must give at least 30 days' written notice before the end of the term if they will not renew.

SECTION 10. NOTICES
Notices to Owner: Mangrove Point Residential LLC, Attn: Community Manager, 8800 Mangrove Point Parkway, Naples, Florida 34108.
