OFFICE LEASE

This Office Lease is entered into between Westshore Commerce Center, LLC ("Landlord") and Brightline Analytics, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES. Landlord leases to Tenant Suite 410 of the Westshore Commerce Center, 3200 W. Cypress Street, Tampa, Florida 33607, consisting of approximately 4,850 rentable square feet of office space, for use as general business offices and for no other purpose. No part of the Premises may be used as a residence or for sleeping.

2. TERM. Five years, commencing February 1, 2027.

3. BASE RENT. $11,920.00 per month, plus Tenant's proportionate share of operating expenses, payable in advance on the first day of each month, together with applicable Florida sales tax.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall deposit $23,840.00 with Landlord as security. Landlord may commingle the security deposit with its other funds and shall not owe Tenant interest on it.

5. DEFAULT. If Tenant fails to pay rent within 2 days after written notice, Landlord may terminate this Lease, change the locks, and re-enter the Premises.

6. UTILITIES. Landlord may discontinue HVAC service after business hours and may suspend utility service to the Premises during any period of monetary default.

7. INDEMNITY AND WAIVER. Landlord shall not be liable for any loss or damage to Tenant's property or business from any cause. Tenant waives any right to recover attorney fees from Landlord.

8. SIGNAGE. Tenant may install one suite sign in the building standard. No flags or banners may be displayed in the windows.

9. ACCESS. Landlord may enter the Premises at any time to inspect or make repairs.

10. NOTICES. Notices to Landlord: Westshore Commerce Center, LLC, 3200 W. Cypress Street, Suite 100, Tampa, Florida 33607.
