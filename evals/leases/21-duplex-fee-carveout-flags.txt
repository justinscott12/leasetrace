TWO-FAMILY DWELLING LEASE

This lease is between Orchard Street Properties LLC (Landlord) and Wen Zhao and Farah Haddad (Tenants) for the upstairs unit of the duplex at 140 Orchard Street, Ocala, Florida 34471.

TERM: 12 months beginning June 1, 2028.
RENT: $1,450 per month.

SECURITY DEPOSIT: Tenants have paid a $1,450 security deposit. Landlord has posted a surety bond with the Marion County clerk of court as provided by law instead of holding the deposit in a separate account. Tenants will receive interest on the deposit at 5 percent per year simple interest. The deposit will be returned within 15 days after the lease ends if no claim is made, and any claim will be noticed in writing within 30 days after the lease ends.

YOUR RENTAL AGREEMENT REQUIRES PAYMENT OF CERTAIN DEPOSITS. THE LANDLORD MAY TRANSFER ADVANCE RENTS TO THE LANDLORD'S ACCOUNT AS THEY ARE DUE AND WITHOUT NOTICE. WHEN YOU MOVE OUT, YOU MUST GIVE THE LANDLORD YOUR NEW ADDRESS SO THAT THE LANDLORD CAN SEND YOU NOTICES REGARDING YOUR DEPOSIT. THE LANDLORD MUST PROVIDE YOU WRITTEN NOTICE IN PERSON, BY MAIL, OR BY E-MAIL IN ACCORDANCE WITH SECTION 83.505, FLORIDA STATUTES, WITHIN 30 DAYS AFTER YOU MOVE OUT, OF THE LANDLORD'S INTENT TO IMPOSE A CLAIM AGAINST THE DEPOSIT. IF YOU DO NOT REPLY TO THE LANDLORD STATING YOUR OBJECTION TO THE CLAIM WITHIN 15 DAYS AFTER RECEIPT OF THE LANDLORD'S WRITTEN NOTICE, THE LANDLORD WILL COLLECT THE CLAIM AND MUST MAIL YOU THE REMAINING DEPOSIT, IF ANY. IF THE LANDLORD FAILS TO TIMELY PROVIDE YOU NOTICE, THE LANDLORD MUST RETURN THE DEPOSIT BUT MAY LATER FILE A LAWSUIT AGAINST YOU FOR DAMAGES. IF YOU FAIL TO TIMELY OBJECT TO A CLAIM, THE LANDLORD MAY COLLECT FROM THE DEPOSIT, BUT YOU MAY LATER FILE A LAWSUIT CLAIMING A REFUND. YOU SHOULD ATTEMPT TO INFORMALLY RESOLVE ANY DISPUTE BEFORE FILING A LAWSUIT. GENERALLY, THE PARTY IN WHOSE FAVOR A JUDGMENT IS RENDERED WILL BE AWARDED COSTS AND ATTORNEY FEES PAYABLE BY THE LOSING PARTY. THIS DISCLOSURE IS BASIC. PLEASE REFER TO PART II OF CHAPTER 83, FLORIDA STATUTES, TO DETERMINE YOUR LEGAL RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS.

REPAIRS: Because the premises is a duplex, Landlord and Tenants agree in writing that Tenants will repair and maintain the plumbing fixtures and drains in the upstairs unit. Landlord will maintain the roof, foundation, and exterior walls.

FLAGS: No flags of any nation, including the United States flag, may be displayed from the premises.

ATTORNEY FEES: The prevailing party in any action may recover reasonable attorney fees, except that no attorney fees shall be awarded in any action in which punitive or exemplary damages are sought.

ENTRY: Landlord may enter for repairs after 24 hours' notice between 8:00 a.m. and 8:00 p.m.

DEFAULT: Landlord may give a written 3-day notice to pay rent or vacate, excluding Saturday, Sunday, and legal holidays.

NON-RENEWAL: The party not renewing must give written notice at least 30 days before the end of the term.

NOTICES: Orchard Street Properties LLC, 2300 SE 17th Street, Suite 2, Ocala, Florida 34471.
