APARTMENT LEASE
Sunbelt Gardens - Building C

Landlord: Sunbelt Gardens Partners, Ltd.
Resident: Marcus Oyelaran
Unit: C-108, Sunbelt Gardens, 1270 Kestrel Way, Orlando, FL 32807 (an apartment in a 140-unit complex)
Lease Term: 12 months, from August 1, 2026 through July 31, 2027
Monthly Rent: $1,540.00, due on the 1st

ARTICLE 1 - DEPOSIT
1.1 Resident shall pay a security deposit of $1,540.00 at signing.
1.2 Landlord may deposit the security deposit in its general operating account and may use those funds for property expenses during the Lease Term.
1.3 Landlord will refund the deposit, less any lawful deductions, within 30 days after Resident vacates if no claim is made.
1.4 If Landlord intends to claim any part of the deposit, Landlord will notify Resident in writing within 45 days after Resident vacates.

ARTICLE 2 - RENT AND DEFAULT
2.1 A late fee of $100.00 applies to any rent received after the 3rd of the month.
2.2 If rent remains unpaid, Landlord may terminate this Lease after giving Resident a 3-day written notice; the 3 days are calendar days and include Saturdays, Sundays, and holidays.
2.3 If rent is more than 10 days late, Landlord may change the locks to the Unit until all amounts owed are paid in full.

ARTICLE 3 - UTILITIES
3.1 Resident is responsible for electricity and internet service.
3.2 Water and sewer are furnished by Landlord through a master meter and billed back to Resident monthly.
3.3 Landlord may shut off water service to the Unit if Resident's account is more than 15 days past due.

ARTICLE 4 - ACCESS
4.1 Landlord will give Resident at least 24 hours' notice before entering to make repairs, and will enter for repairs only between 9:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m., except in an emergency.

ARTICLE 5 - MAINTENANCE
5.1 Landlord will maintain the roof, structure, plumbing, and common areas and will comply with applicable housing codes.
5.2 Resident will promptly report leaks and will pay for repairs to damage Resident or Resident's guests cause.

ARTICLE 6 - RENEWAL
6.1 Either party may end this Lease at the end of the term by giving written notice at least 30 days before July 31, 2027.

ARTICLE 7 - NOTICES
7.1 Notices to Landlord shall be sent to Sunbelt Gardens Partners, Ltd., 1270 Kestrel Way, Office 1, Orlando, FL 32807.

ARTICLE 8 - FEES
8.1 In any action arising from this Lease, the prevailing party shall be entitled to reasonable attorney fees.
