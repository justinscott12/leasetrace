RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT
Palmetto Crossing Apartments

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Lease") is made on June 2, 2026, between Palmetto Crossing Holdings, LLC ("Landlord") and Dana Whitfield ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES. Landlord leases to Tenant Apartment 214 at Palmetto Crossing Apartments, 4410 Heron Bay Drive, Sarasota, Florida 34233 (the "Premises"). The Premises is an apartment in a 96-unit residential community.

2. TERM. The term of this Lease begins on July 1, 2026 and ends at 11:59 p.m. on June 30, 2027.

3. RENT. Tenant will pay monthly rent of $1,685.00, due on the first day of each month, through the resident portal or by check delivered to the leasing office.

4. LATE CHARGES. If rent is not received by the fifth day of the month, Tenant will owe a late charge of $75.00.

5. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant has paid a security deposit of $1,685.00. Landlord will hold the deposit in a separate non-interest-bearing account at Gulfline Community Bank, 900 Main Street, Sarasota, Florida 34236. Tenant is not entitled to interest on the deposit. If Landlord does not intend to impose a claim on the deposit, Landlord will return it within 15 days after this Lease ends. If Landlord intends to impose a claim, Landlord will send written notice of the claim by certified mail within 30 days after this Lease ends.

YOUR RENTAL AGREEMENT REQUIRES PAYMENT OF CERTAIN DEPOSITS. THE LANDLORD MAY TRANSFER ADVANCE RENTS TO THE LANDLORD'S ACCOUNT AS THEY ARE DUE AND WITHOUT NOTICE. WHEN YOU MOVE OUT, YOU MUST GIVE THE LANDLORD YOUR NEW ADDRESS SO THAT THE LANDLORD CAN SEND YOU NOTICES REGARDING YOUR DEPOSIT. THE LANDLORD MUST PROVIDE YOU WRITTEN NOTICE IN PERSON, BY MAIL, OR BY E-MAIL IN ACCORDANCE WITH SECTION 83.505, FLORIDA STATUTES, WITHIN 30 DAYS AFTER YOU MOVE OUT, OF THE LANDLORD'S INTENT TO IMPOSE A CLAIM AGAINST THE DEPOSIT. IF YOU DO NOT REPLY TO THE LANDLORD STATING YOUR OBJECTION TO THE CLAIM WITHIN 15 DAYS AFTER RECEIPT OF THE LANDLORD'S WRITTEN NOTICE, THE LANDLORD WILL COLLECT THE CLAIM AND MUST MAIL YOU THE REMAINING DEPOSIT, IF ANY. IF THE LANDLORD FAILS TO TIMELY PROVIDE YOU NOTICE, THE LANDLORD MUST RETURN THE DEPOSIT BUT MAY LATER FILE A LAWSUIT AGAINST YOU FOR DAMAGES. IF YOU FAIL TO TIMELY OBJECT TO A CLAIM, THE LANDLORD MAY COLLECT FROM THE DEPOSIT, BUT YOU MAY LATER FILE A LAWSUIT CLAIMING A REFUND. YOU SHOULD ATTEMPT TO INFORMALLY RESOLVE ANY DISPUTE BEFORE FILING A LAWSUIT. GENERALLY, THE PARTY IN WHOSE FAVOR A JUDGMENT IS RENDERED WILL BE AWARDED COSTS AND ATTORNEY FEES PAYABLE BY THE LOSING PARTY. THIS DISCLOSURE IS BASIC. PLEASE REFER TO PART II OF CHAPTER 83, FLORIDA STATUTES, TO DETERMINE YOUR LEGAL RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS.

6. UNPAID RENT. If rent is unpaid, Landlord may deliver a written notice demanding payment or possession within 3 days, excluding Saturday, Sunday, and legal holidays, before terminating this Lease.

7. LANDLORD'S ACCESS. Landlord may enter the Premises to inspect, make repairs, or show the Premises after giving Tenant at least 24 hours' notice, and only between 8:00 a.m. and 7:00 p.m. Landlord may enter at any time in an emergency or to protect or preserve the Premises.

8. MAINTENANCE. Landlord will comply with applicable building, housing, and health codes and will keep the roof, windows, exterior walls, foundation, and plumbing in good repair. Tenant will keep the Premises clean and sanitary, replace light bulbs and air filters, and reimburse Landlord for the cost of repairing damage caused by Tenant, occupants, or guests.

9. LOCKS. Tenant may not add or change locks without Landlord's written consent. Landlord will rekey the Premises before move-in.

10. FLAGS AND DECORATIONS. Tenant may display one portable, removable United States flag in a respectful manner. Other exterior decorations require approval from the leasing office.

11. PETS. No pets are allowed without a signed pet addendum and a $300 nonrefundable pet fee.

12. ENDING THE LEASE. Either party who does not intend to renew must give the other written notice at least 60 days before the end of the term.

13. ATTORNEY FEES. In any lawsuit to enforce this Lease or Part II of Chapter 83, Florida Statutes, the prevailing party may recover reasonable attorney fees and court costs.

14. NOTICES. Notices to Landlord must be delivered to Palmetto Crossing Holdings, LLC, Leasing Office, 4410 Heron Bay Drive, Sarasota, Florida 34233.
