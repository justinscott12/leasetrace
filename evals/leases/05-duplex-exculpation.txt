DUPLEX LEASE

1. Parties and Premises
Lessor: Coastal Duplex Rentals, Inc. Lessee: Harold and June Bessemer. The premises are the east half of the duplex at 2405 Seagrape Street, Fort Pierce, Florida 34950.

2. Term and Rent
The lease term is six months, from November 1, 2026 to April 30, 2027. Rent is $1,375.00 per month.

3. Security Deposit
Lessee shall deposit $1,375.00 as security. Lessor will hold the security deposit in a separate interest-bearing account at Treasure Coast Federal Credit Union, 501 Orange Avenue, Fort Pierce, Florida 34950. Lessee will receive interest at 75 percent of the annualized average interest rate payable on the account, or 5 percent per year simple interest, at Lessor's election. The deposit will be returned within 15 days after the lease ends if Lessor makes no claim, and notice of any claim will be sent within 30 days after the lease ends.

YOUR RENTAL AGREEMENT REQUIRES PAYMENT OF CERTAIN DEPOSITS. THE LANDLORD MAY TRANSFER ADVANCE RENTS TO THE LANDLORD'S ACCOUNT AS THEY ARE DUE AND WITHOUT NOTICE. WHEN YOU MOVE OUT, YOU MUST GIVE THE LANDLORD YOUR NEW ADDRESS SO THAT THE LANDLORD CAN SEND YOU NOTICES REGARDING YOUR DEPOSIT. THE LANDLORD MUST PROVIDE YOU WRITTEN NOTICE IN PERSON, BY MAIL, OR BY E-MAIL IN ACCORDANCE WITH SECTION 83.505, FLORIDA STATUTES, WITHIN 30 DAYS AFTER YOU MOVE OUT, OF THE LANDLORD'S INTENT TO IMPOSE A CLAIM AGAINST THE DEPOSIT. IF YOU DO NOT REPLY TO THE LANDLORD STATING YOUR OBJECTION TO THE CLAIM WITHIN 15 DAYS AFTER RECEIPT OF THE LANDLORD'S WRITTEN NOTICE, THE LANDLORD WILL COLLECT THE CLAIM AND MUST MAIL YOU THE REMAINING DEPOSIT, IF ANY. IF THE LANDLORD FAILS TO TIMELY PROVIDE YOU NOTICE, THE LANDLORD MUST RETURN THE DEPOSIT BUT MAY LATER FILE A LAWSUIT AGAINST YOU FOR DAMAGES. IF YOU FAIL TO TIMELY OBJECT TO A CLAIM, THE LANDLORD MAY COLLECT FROM THE DEPOSIT, BUT YOU MAY LATER FILE A LAWSUIT CLAIMING A REFUND. YOU SHOULD ATTEMPT TO INFORMALLY RESOLVE ANY DISPUTE BEFORE FILING A LAWSUIT. GENERALLY, THE PARTY IN WHOSE FAVOR A JUDGMENT IS RENDERED WILL BE AWARDED COSTS AND ATTORNEY FEES PAYABLE BY THE LOSING PARTY. THIS DISCLOSURE IS BASIC. PLEASE REFER TO PART II OF CHAPTER 83, FLORIDA STATUTES, TO DETERMINE YOUR LEGAL RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS.

4. Repairs
As permitted for a duplex, Lessee agrees in writing to make and pay for repairs to the plumbing serving the east unit, including the water heater. Lessor will keep the roof, foundation, and exterior walls in good repair.

5. Entry
Lessor may enter the premises to make repairs after giving Lessee at least 12 hours' notice, between 8:00 a.m. and 8:00 p.m.

6. Liability
Lessor shall not be liable to Lessee, Lessee's family, or guests for any damage, injury, or loss to person or property from any cause whatsoever, including Lessor's own negligence.

Lessor is not responsible for loss or damage caused by the criminal acts of third parties, including burglary and vandalism.

7. Default
If rent is unpaid, Lessor may serve a written three-day notice (excluding Saturday, Sunday, and legal holidays) to pay rent or deliver possession.

8. Notices
All notices to Lessor shall be mailed to Coastal Duplex Rentals, Inc., P.O. Box 1188, Fort Pierce, Florida 34954.

9. Pets
One cat is permitted. Dogs are not allowed.
