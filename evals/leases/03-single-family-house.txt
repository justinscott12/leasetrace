SINGLE-FAMILY HOME RENTAL AGREEMENT

Owner: Gail and Robert Tremont ("Owner")
Tenant: Priya Ramaswamy ("Tenant")
Property: 618 Live Oak Lane, Gainesville, Florida 32605, a detached single-family home with a two-car garage (the "Home").

TERM. This Agreement runs for one year, starting September 1, 2026 and ending August 31, 2027.

RENT. $2,150 per month, payable by the 1st of each month by bank transfer to the account Owner designates.

SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $2,150. Owner will keep the deposit in a separate interest-bearing account at Alachua Savings Bank, 22 University Avenue, Gainesville, Florida 32601, and Tenant is entitled to interest on the deposit at the rate the account pays, credited annually. Owner will return the deposit with interest within 15 days after the Agreement ends if Owner makes no claim, and will give written notice of any claim within 30 days after the Agreement ends.

YOUR RENTAL AGREEMENT REQUIRES PAYMENT OF CERTAIN DEPOSITS. THE LANDLORD MAY TRANSFER ADVANCE RENTS TO THE LANDLORD'S ACCOUNT AS THEY ARE DUE AND WITHOUT NOTICE. WHEN YOU MOVE OUT, YOU MUST GIVE THE LANDLORD YOUR NEW ADDRESS SO THAT THE LANDLORD CAN SEND YOU NOTICES REGARDING YOUR DEPOSIT. THE LANDLORD MUST PROVIDE YOU WRITTEN NOTICE IN PERSON, BY MAIL, OR BY E-MAIL IN ACCORDANCE WITH SECTION 83.505, FLORIDA STATUTES, WITHIN 30 DAYS AFTER YOU MOVE OUT, OF THE LANDLORD'S INTENT TO IMPOSE A CLAIM AGAINST THE DEPOSIT. IF YOU DO NOT REPLY TO THE LANDLORD STATING YOUR OBJECTION TO THE CLAIM WITHIN 15 DAYS AFTER RECEIPT OF THE LANDLORD'S WRITTEN NOTICE, THE LANDLORD WILL COLLECT THE CLAIM AND MUST MAIL YOU THE REMAINING DEPOSIT, IF ANY. IF THE LANDLORD FAILS TO TIMELY PROVIDE YOU NOTICE, THE LANDLORD MUST RETURN THE DEPOSIT BUT MAY LATER FILE A LAWSUIT AGAINST YOU FOR DAMAGES. IF YOU FAIL TO TIMELY OBJECT TO A CLAIM, THE LANDLORD MAY COLLECT FROM THE DEPOSIT, BUT YOU MAY LATER FILE A LAWSUIT CLAIMING A REFUND. YOU SHOULD ATTEMPT TO INFORMALLY RESOLVE ANY DISPUTE BEFORE FILING A LAWSUIT. GENERALLY, THE PARTY IN WHOSE FAVOR A JUDGMENT IS RENDERED WILL BE AWARDED COSTS AND ATTORNEY FEES PAYABLE BY THE LOSING PARTY. THIS DISCLOSURE IS BASIC. PLEASE REFER TO PART II OF CHAPTER 83, FLORIDA STATUTES, TO DETERMINE YOUR LEGAL RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS.

REPAIRS AND UPKEEP. Because the Home is a single-family residence, Owner and Tenant agree in writing that Tenant is responsible for routine repairs to the plumbing, including clogged drains, running toilets, and leaking faucets, and for minor roof repairs costing less than $500 per repair. Owner remains responsible for the foundation, exterior walls, and any roof replacement. Tenant shall mow the lawn and keep the gutters clear.

HOA. The Home is in the Tremont Ridge Homeowners Association. Tenant shall follow the HOA's parking and trash rules and pay any HOA fine caused by Tenant's conduct.

ENTRY. Owner may enter the Home to make repairs on 24 hours' notice, between 8:00 a.m. and 6:00 p.m.

NONPAYMENT. If rent is not paid when due, Owner may give Tenant a written 3-day notice, excluding weekends and court-observed holidays, demanding payment or possession.

NOTICE BEFORE MOVING OUT. Tenant must give Owner written notice at least 90 days before the end of the term if Tenant will not renew. Owner must give Tenant at least 30 days' written notice of non-renewal.

ATTORNEY FEES. Each party shall bear its own attorney fees and costs in any dispute arising out of this Agreement.
