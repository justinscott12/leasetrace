RESIDENTIAL TOWNHOME LEASE

PARTIES. Heritage Townhomes Management, LLC ("Management") leases to Elena Marchetti ("Resident") townhome No. 22 at Heritage Townhomes, 455 Cedar Ridge Court, Jacksonville, Florida 32256. Heritage Townhomes is a community of 36 attached townhomes managed as one rental property.

TERM. Six months, from June 1, 2027 through November 30, 2027.

RENT. $1,890 per month.

DEPOSIT. Resident has paid a $1,890 security deposit, held in a separate non-interest-bearing account at Duval Riverside Bank, 76 S. Laura Street, Jacksonville, Florida 32202. Resident will not earn interest on the deposit. Management will return the deposit within 15 days after the lease ends if it makes no claim, and will send notice of any claim within 30 days after the lease ends.

YOUR RENTAL AGREEMENT REQUIRES PAYMENT OF CERTAIN DEPOSITS. THE LANDLORD MAY TRANSFER ADVANCE RENTS TO THE LANDLORD'S ACCOUNT AS THEY ARE DUE AND WITHOUT NOTICE. WHEN YOU MOVE OUT, YOU MUST GIVE THE LANDLORD YOUR NEW ADDRESS SO THAT THE LANDLORD CAN SEND YOU NOTICES REGARDING YOUR DEPOSIT. THE LANDLORD MUST PROVIDE YOU WRITTEN NOTICE IN PERSON, BY MAIL, OR BY E-MAIL IN ACCORDANCE WITH SECTION 83.505, FLORIDA STATUTES, WITHIN 30 DAYS AFTER YOU MOVE OUT, OF THE LANDLORD'S INTENT TO IMPOSE A CLAIM AGAINST THE DEPOSIT. IF YOU DO NOT REPLY TO THE LANDLORD STATING YOUR OBJECTION TO THE CLAIM WITHIN 15 DAYS AFTER RECEIPT OF THE LANDLORD'S WRITTEN NOTICE, THE LANDLORD WILL COLLECT THE CLAIM AND MUST MAIL YOU THE REMAINING DEPOSIT, IF ANY. IF THE LANDLORD FAILS TO TIMELY PROVIDE YOU NOTICE, THE LANDLORD MUST RETURN THE DEPOSIT BUT MAY LATER FILE A LAWSUIT AGAINST YOU FOR DAMAGES. IF YOU FAIL TO TIMELY OBJECT TO A CLAIM, THE LANDLORD MAY COLLECT FROM THE DEPOSIT, BUT YOU MAY LATER FILE A LAWSUIT CLAIMING A REFUND. YOU SHOULD ATTEMPT TO INFORMALLY RESOLVE ANY DISPUTE BEFORE FILING A LAWSUIT. GENERALLY, THE PARTY IN WHOSE FAVOR A JUDGMENT IS RENDERED WILL BE AWARDED COSTS AND ATTORNEY FEES PAYABLE BY THE LOSING PARTY. THIS DISCLOSURE IS BASIC. PLEASE REFER TO PART II OF CHAPTER 83, FLORIDA STATUTES, TO DETERMINE YOUR LEGAL RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS.

DAMAGE. Resident shall reimburse Management for the reasonable cost of repairing any damage to the townhome, its fixtures, or its plumbing caused by Resident, Resident's occupants, or guests, including damage caused by their negligence or misuse. Normal wear and tear excepted.

MAINTENANCE. Management will comply with applicable building, housing, and health codes and keep the structure and plumbing in good repair.

UTILITIES. Management may interrupt utility service to the townhome as permitted by Florida statute.

LEGAL FEES. If Management brings any action against Resident, Resident shall pay Management's attorney fees and costs. Resident shall not be entitled to recover attorney fees from Management in any action.

ENTRY. Management may enter for repairs on 24 hours' notice between 8:00 a.m. and 6:00 p.m.

NONPAYMENT. Management may give a written 3-day notice to pay rent or vacate, excluding weekends and legal holidays.

GUESTS. Guests may not stay more than 14 days in any 6-month period without written approval.

NOTICES. Send notices to Heritage Townhomes Management, LLC, 455 Cedar Ridge Court, Suite A, Jacksonville, Florida 32256.
