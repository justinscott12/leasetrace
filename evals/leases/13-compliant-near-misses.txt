RESIDENTIAL LEASE - LAKESIDE COMMONS

Lakeside Commons Residential, LLC ("we" or "us") leases to Rosa and Daniel Fuentes ("you") Apartment 1204 at Lakeside Commons, 2300 Lakeside Commons Drive, Kissimmee, Florida 34741, an apartment in a 228-unit community.

PART 1 - BASIC TERMS

1.1 Term. Your lease begins on February 1, 2027 and ends on January 31, 2028.

1.2 Rent. Your monthly rent is $1,725, due on the 1st. You may pay online or at the office.

1.3 Late Fees. Rent received after the 4th is late, and a $60 late fee applies.

1.4 Security Deposit. Your security deposit is $1,725. We hold it in a separate non-interest-bearing account at Osceola Lakes Bank, 101 Broadway, Kissimmee, Florida 34741. You are not entitled to interest on the deposit. We will return it within 15 days after you move out if we make no claim, and we will send any claim notice by certified mail within 30 days after you move out.

YOUR RENTAL AGREEMENT REQUIRES PAYMENT OF CERTAIN DEPOSITS. THE LANDLORD MAY TRANSFER ADVANCE RENTS TO THE LANDLORD'S ACCOUNT AS THEY ARE DUE AND WITHOUT NOTICE. WHEN YOU MOVE OUT, YOU MUST GIVE THE LANDLORD YOUR NEW ADDRESS SO THAT THE LANDLORD CAN SEND YOU NOTICES REGARDING YOUR DEPOSIT. THE LANDLORD MUST PROVIDE YOU WRITTEN NOTICE IN PERSON, BY MAIL, OR BY E-MAIL IN ACCORDANCE WITH SECTION 83.505, FLORIDA STATUTES, WITHIN 30 DAYS AFTER YOU MOVE OUT, OF THE LANDLORD'S INTENT TO IMPOSE A CLAIM AGAINST THE DEPOSIT. IF YOU DO NOT REPLY TO THE LANDLORD STATING YOUR OBJECTION TO THE CLAIM WITHIN 15 DAYS AFTER RECEIPT OF THE LANDLORD'S WRITTEN NOTICE, THE LANDLORD WILL COLLECT THE CLAIM AND MUST MAIL YOU THE REMAINING DEPOSIT, IF ANY. IF THE LANDLORD FAILS TO TIMELY PROVIDE YOU NOTICE, THE LANDLORD MUST RETURN THE DEPOSIT BUT MAY LATER FILE A LAWSUIT AGAINST YOU FOR DAMAGES. IF YOU FAIL TO TIMELY OBJECT TO A CLAIM, THE LANDLORD MAY COLLECT FROM THE DEPOSIT, BUT YOU MAY LATER FILE A LAWSUIT CLAIMING A REFUND. YOU SHOULD ATTEMPT TO INFORMALLY RESOLVE ANY DISPUTE BEFORE FILING A LAWSUIT. GENERALLY, THE PARTY IN WHOSE FAVOR A JUDGMENT IS RENDERED WILL BE AWARDED COSTS AND ATTORNEY FEES PAYABLE BY THE LOSING PARTY. THIS DISCLOSURE IS BASIC. PLEASE REFER TO PART II OF CHAPTER 83, FLORIDA STATUTES, TO DETERMINE YOUR LEGAL RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS.

PART 2 - PAYMENT DEFAULT

2.1 If you don't pay rent when due, we may deliver a written notice requiring payment or possession within 3 days, not counting Saturdays, Sundays, or legal holidays. We may only recover possession of the apartment through a court eviction.

PART 3 - ACCESS AND REPAIRS

3.1 We will give you at least 24 hours' notice before entering to make repairs, and we will enter for repairs only between 8:00 a.m. and 8:00 p.m.

3.2 We may enter at any time in an emergency or to protect or preserve the premises.

3.3 We will comply with applicable building, housing, and health codes and keep the roof, structure, and plumbing in good repair. You must keep the apartment clean, use plumbing fixtures properly, and pay for any repair needed because of damage you, your occupants, or your guests cause, including clogged drains caused by improper use.

3.4 We may need to shut off water to your building briefly when we repair a main line. We will post notice at least 24 hours ahead when practical and restore service as soon as the repair is complete.

PART 4 - SECURITY AND LIABILITY

4.1 We are not responsible for loss or injury caused by the criminal acts of other people, such as theft or vandalism, unless caused by our failure to meet a duty the law imposes on us.

4.2 Nothing in this lease limits either party's liability to the other under law.

4.3 You may not change or add locks without our written consent, and you must give us a key to any lock we approve.

PART 5 - RULES

5.1 Pets. Up to two pets are allowed with a pet addendum and a monthly pet rent of $35 per pet.

5.2 Flags. You may display one portable, removable United States flag, no larger than 4 1/2 feet by 6 feet, in a respectful manner. Political signs and banners may not be attached to the building exterior.

5.3 Complaints. You may report conditions in the apartment to any government agency. We will not raise your rent, reduce services, or threaten eviction because you did so.

PART 6 - MOVING OUT

6.1 If you or we do not intend to renew, the party not renewing must give written notice at least 60 days before the end of the term.

6.2 BY SIGNING THIS RENTAL AGREEMENT, THE TENANT AGREES THAT UPON SURRENDER, ABANDONMENT, OR RECOVERY OF POSSESSION OF THE DWELLING UNIT DUE TO THE DEATH OF THE LAST REMAINING TENANT, AS PROVIDED BY CHAPTER 83, FLORIDA STATUTES, THE LANDLORD SHALL NOT BE LIABLE OR RESPONSIBLE FOR STORAGE OR DISPOSITION OF THE TENANT'S PERSONAL PROPERTY.

6.3 We will only remove personal property you leave in the apartment after you surrender or abandon it, or after a lawful eviction.

PART 7 - FEES AND NOTICES

7.1 In any lawsuit about this lease or Part II of Chapter 83, Florida Statutes, the prevailing party may recover reasonable attorney fees and court costs.

7.2 Send notices to us at Lakeside Commons Residential, LLC, Management Office, 2300 Lakeside Commons Drive, Kissimmee, Florida 34741.
