LEASE - MAGNOLIA COURT APARTMENTS

1. PARTIES. Magnolia Court Apartments, LLC ("Landlord") and Benjamin Achterberg ("Tenant").

2. UNIT. Apartment 2C, Magnolia Court Apartments, 815 W. Garden Street, Pensacola, Florida 32502, in a 30-unit apartment building.

3. TERM. January 15, 2028 through January 14, 2029.

4. RENT. $1,275 per month.

5. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant has paid $1,275 as a security deposit. Landlord holds the deposit in a separate non-interest-bearing account at Emerald Coast Bank, 33 Palafox Place, Pensacola, Florida 32502. Tenant is not entitled to interest. Landlord will return the deposit within 10 days after the lease ends if it makes no claim, and will give written notice of any claim within 30 days after the lease ends.

YOUR RENTAL AGREEMENT REQUIRES PAYMENT OF CERTAIN DEPOSITS. THE LANDLORD MAY TRANSFER ADVANCE RENTS TO THE LANDLORD'S ACCOUNT AS THEY ARE DUE AND WITHOUT NOTICE. WHEN YOU MOVE OUT, YOU MUST GIVE THE LANDLORD YOUR NEW ADDRESS SO THAT THE LANDLORD CAN SEND YOU NOTICES REGARDING YOUR DEPOSIT. THE LANDLORD MUST PROVIDE YOU WRITTEN NOTICE IN PERSON, BY MAIL, OR BY E-MAIL IN ACCORDANCE WITH SECTION 83.505, FLORIDA STATUTES, WITHIN 30 DAYS AFTER YOU MOVE OUT, OF THE LANDLORD'S INTENT TO IMPOSE A CLAIM AGAINST THE DEPOSIT. IF YOU DO NOT REPLY TO THE LANDLORD STATING YOUR OBJECTION TO THE CLAIM WITHIN 15 DAYS AFTER RECEIPT OF THE LANDLORD'S WRITTEN NOTICE, THE LANDLORD WILL COLLECT THE CLAIM AND MUST MAIL YOU THE REMAINING DEPOSIT, IF ANY. IF THE LANDLORD FAILS TO TIMELY PROVIDE YOU NOTICE, THE LANDLORD MUST RETURN THE DEPOSIT BUT MAY LATER FILE A LAWSUIT AGAINST YOU FOR DAMAGES. IF YOU FAIL TO TIMELY OBJECT TO A CLAIM, THE LANDLORD MAY COLLECT FROM THE DEPOSIT, BUT YOU MAY LATER FILE A LAWSUIT CLAIMING A REFUND. YOU SHOULD ATTEMPT TO INFORMALLY RESOLVE ANY DISPUTE BEFORE FILING A LAWSUIT. GENERALLY, THE PARTY IN WHOSE FAVOR A JUDGMENT IS RENDERED WILL BE AWARDED COSTS AND ATTORNEY FEES PAYABLE BY THE LOSING PARTY. THIS DISCLOSURE IS BASIC. PLEASE REFER TO PART II OF CHAPTER 83, FLORIDA STATUTES, TO DETERMINE YOUR LEGAL RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS.

6. CODE COMPLAINTS. Tenant agrees not to contact code enforcement or any other governmental agency about the condition of the Unit without first giving Landlord 30 days to address the issue. Any such contact before then is a default under this lease, and Landlord may terminate the lease.

7. LATE RENT AND PROPERTY. If rent is more than 15 days late, Landlord may remove Tenant's belongings from the Unit and hold them until Tenant pays all rent due.

8. ABANDONED PROPERTY. After Tenant surrenders or abandons the Unit, or after a lawful eviction, Landlord may remove and dispose of any personal property Tenant leaves behind.

BY SIGNING THIS RENTAL AGREEMENT, THE TENANT AGREES THAT UPON SURRENDER, ABANDONMENT, OR RECOVERY OF POSSESSION OF THE DWELLING UNIT DUE TO THE DEATH OF THE LAST REMAINING TENANT, AS PROVIDED BY CHAPTER 83, FLORIDA STATUTES, THE LANDLORD SHALL NOT BE LIABLE OR RESPONSIBLE FOR STORAGE OR DISPOSITION OF THE TENANT'S PERSONAL PROPERTY.

9. NOTICE OF NON-RENEWAL. Tenant must notify Landlord in writing at least 15 days before the end of the term if Tenant will not renew.

10. ENTRY. Landlord may enter to make repairs with 24 hours' notice between 8:00 a.m. and 8:00 p.m.

11. NONPAYMENT. If rent is unpaid, Landlord may serve a written 3-day notice, excluding Saturday, Sunday, and legal holidays.

12. NOTICES. Notices to Landlord: Magnolia Court Apartments, LLC, 815 W. Garden Street, Office, Pensacola, Florida 32502.
