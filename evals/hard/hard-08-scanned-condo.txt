CONDOMINIUM LEASE - BAYVIEW TERRACE

Unit Owner: Whitcombe Holdings, LLC ("Unit Owner"). Renter: Jonah Feldstein ("Renter").
Unit: Unit 806, Bayview Terrace Condominium, 700 Bayshore Drive, Fort Lauderdale,
Florida 33304, a 140-unit condominium building (the "Unit"). Term: April 1, 2029 to
March 31, 2030. Rent: $2,950.00 per month.

1. Payment of Rent. Rent is payable in advance, without demand, on or before the first
day of each month through the resident portal or by money order or cashier's check
delivered to the leasing office. Unit Owner may require certified funds after any
returned payment. Partial payments may be accepted without waiving Unit Owner's rights,
as permitted by law.

2. Late Charges. If any rent is not received by 11:59 p.m. on the third day of the
month, Renter will pay an initial late charge of $75.00 plus $10.00 per day thereafter
until paid, not to exceed $150.00 in total for any month. A $50.00 fee applies to any
returned payment.

3. Security Deposit. Renter has paid a security deposit of $2,950.00, which Unit Owner
holds in a separate non-interest-bearing account at New River Savings Bank, 100 SE 3rd
Avenue, Fort Lauderdale, Florida 33301. Renter is not entitled to interest. The deposit
will be returned within 15 days after the Lease ends if no claim is made, and notice of
any claim will be sent within 30 days after the Lease ends.

YOUR RENTAL AGREEMENT REQUIRES PAYMENT OF CERTAIN DEPOSITS. THE LANDLORD MAY TRANSFER
ADVANCE RENTS TO THE LANDLORD'S ACCOUNT AS THEY ARE DUE AND WITHOUT NOTICE. WHEN YOU
MOVE OUT, YOU MUST GIVE THE LANDLORD YOUR NEW ADDRESS SO THAT THE LANDLORD CAN SEND YOU
NOTICES REGARDING YOUR DEPOSIT. THE LANDLORD MUST PROVIDE YOU WRITTEN NOTICE IN PERSON,
BY MAIL, OR BY E-MAIL IN ACCORDANCE WITH SECTION 83.505, FLORIDA STATUTES, WITHIN 30
DAYS AFTER YOU MOVE OUT, OF THE LANDLORD'S INTENT TO IMPOSE A CLAIM AGAINST THE DEPOSIT.
IF YOU DO NOT REPLY TO THE LANDLORD STATING YOUR OBJECTION TO THE CLAIM WITHIN 15 DAYS
AFTER RECEIPT OF THE LANDLORD'S WRITTEN NOTICE, THE LANDLORD WILL COLLECT THE CLAIM AND
MUST MAIL YOU THE REMAINING DEPOSIT, IF ANY. IF THE LANDLORD FAILS TO TIMELY PROVIDE YOU
NOTICE, THE LANDLORD MUST RETURN THE DEPOSIT BUT MAY LATER FILE A LAWSUIT AGAINST YOU
FOR DAMAGES. IF YOU FAIL TO TIMELY OBJECT TO A CLAIM, THE LANDLORD MAY COLLECT FROM THE
DEPOSIT, BUT YOU MAY LATER FILE A LAWSUIT CLAIMING A REFUND. YOU SHOULD ATTEMPT TO
INFORMALLY RESOLVE ANY DISPUTE BEFORE FILING A LAWSUIT. GENERALLY, THE PARTY IN WHOSE
FAVOR A JUDGMENT IS RENDERED WILL BE AWARDED COSTS AND ATTORNEY FEES PAYABLE BY THE
LOSING PARTY. THIS DISCLOSURE IS BASIC. PLEASE REFER TO PART II OF CHAPTER 83, FLORIDA
STATUTES, TO DETERMINE YOUR LEGAL RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS.

4. Association. Renter must comply with the Declaration of Condominium and the rules of
the Bayview Terrace Condominium Association, including its move-in fee, elevator
reservation and two-vehicle limit. The Association must approve this Lease before
occupancy.

5. Rent Default. If rent is not paid when due, Unit Owner may terminate this Lease after
delivering a written demand and allowing Renter two (2) business days to pay the rent or
deliver possession of the Unit.

6. Occupants. Only the persons named in this Lease as Renter or as authorized occupants
may live in the Unit. Any other person who stays in the Unit for more than the number of
nights allowed in the Community Policies must be approved in writing by Unit Owner and
may be required to complete a rental application. Unit Owner may require a person who
has stayed beyond that period to be added to this Lease as a party.

7. Use of the Premises. The Unit may be used only as a private residence. Renter may not
operate a business in the Unit that involves customers, clients or employees coming to
the Unit, inventory storage, or signage. Working from home with a computer and telephone
is permitted. Renter may not use the Unit for any purpose that violates federal, state
or local law.

8. Conduct. Renter, occupants and guests must not engage in conduct that disturbs other
residents' peaceful enjoyment of the community, including loud music, shouting, or
activity that causes excessive vibration. Quiet hours are from 10:00 p.m. to 8:00 a.m.
Sunday through Thursday and from 11:00 p.m. to 9:00 a.m. on Friday and Saturday nights.
Renter is responsible for the conduct of occupants and guests.

9. Smoking. Smoking of any substance, including tobacco, cannabis and electronic
cigarettes or vaporizers, is prohibited inside the Unit, on balconies and patios, and
within 25 feet of any building entrance. Renter will be charged for the reasonable cost
of removing smoke odor or residue caused by smoking in violation of this section.

10. Renter's Insurance. Renter must obtain and keep in force during the Lease term a
renter's insurance policy with personal liability coverage of at least $100,000 and must
provide proof of coverage before move-in and upon request. Unit Owner's property
insurance covers the building, not Renter's furniture, clothing, electronics or other
belongings, and does not provide liability coverage for Renter.

11. Indemnity and Release. Renter will indemnify Unit Owner against claims arising from
the acts of Renter, occupants and guests. Renter releases Unit Owner and the Association
from all claims for injury, loss or damage to persons or property in or about the Unit
or the condominium, from any cause whatsoever.

12. Moisture and Mold. Florida's climate requires steady air conditioning and
ventilation to control humidity. Renter will keep the air conditioning running at a
setting no higher than 80 degrees, use exhaust fans while bathing and cooking, wipe up
standing water promptly, and report any visible mold, water leaks, or musty odors to
Unit Owner in writing within 24 hours of discovering them.

13. Pest Control. Unit Owner provides routine pest control treatment for common
household insects. Renter will keep the Unit clean and free of food debris and clutter
that attracts pests and will prepare the Unit for scheduled treatments as instructed.
Renter must report any sign of bed bugs immediately and must not attempt to treat bed
bugs with over-the-counter products.

14. Smoke Detectors. The Unit is equipped with smoke detectors. Renter will test the
detectors monthly, will not disconnect or remove them or their batteries, and will
promptly report any detector that does not work.

15. Alterations. Renter may not paint, wallpaper, install fixtures, replace appliances,
or make other alterations to the Unit without Unit Owner's prior written consent. Small
nails for hanging pictures are permitted. Any alteration made with consent becomes part
of the Unit unless Unit Owner agrees otherwise in writing.

16. Appliances. The Unit includes a refrigerator, range, dishwasher, microwave and
washer and dryer connections. Renter will use the appliances only for their intended
purposes and in accordance with the manufacturers' instructions. Renter may not install
additional major appliances, such as a second refrigerator or a portable dishwasher,
without Unit Owner's written consent.

17. Air Conditioning Filters. Renter will replace the air conditioning filter at least
every 60 days with a filter of the size and type specified by Unit Owner, and will keep
the return air vent unobstructed.

18. Hurricanes and Severe Weather. When a hurricane watch or warning is issued for the
area, Renter will bring in all patio and balcony furniture, grills, plants and other
loose items. Renter must follow any evacuation order issued by local authorities. Renter
may not install storm shutters or tape windows without Unit Owner's consent.

19. Entry for Repairs. Unit Owner or its contractors may enter the Unit to make repairs
between 7:00 a.m. and 7:00 p.m., on not less than 24 hours' notice, and at any time in
an emergency.

20. Maintenance. Unit Owner will keep the Unit's plumbing, fixtures and appliances in
good repair and comply with applicable housing codes; the Association maintains the
building structure and roof.

21. Flag. Notwithstanding any Association rule, Renter may display one portable,
removable United States flag, not larger than 4 1/2 feet by 6 feet, in a respectful
manner.

22. Assignment and Subletting. Renter may not assign this Lease or sublet any part of
the Unit, including through short-term rental platforms, without Unit Owner's prior
written consent.

23. Animals. No animals of any kind, including visiting animals, are allowed in the Unit
without a signed animal addendum, except assistance animals approved as required by law.
Approved pets require a nonrefundable pet fee of $350 and monthly pet rent of $35 per
animal.

24. Guests. Guests may not stay in the Unit more than 7 consecutive nights or more than
14 nights in any six-month period without Unit Owner's written consent.

25. Non-Renewal. The party not renewing must give written notice at least 45 days before
the end of the term.

26. Attorney Fees. The prevailing party in any action on this Lease may recover
reasonable attorney fees and costs.

27. Notices. Notices to Unit Owner: Whitcombe Holdings, LLC, 2600 E. Las Olas Boulevard,
Suite 3, Fort Lauderdale, Florida 33301.

28. Jury Trial. To the extent permitted by law, Unit Owner and Renter waive trial by
jury in any lawsuit arising out of this Lease or Renter's occupancy of the Unit.

29. Radon Gas. RADON GAS: Radon is a naturally occurring radioactive gas that, when it
has accumulated in a building in sufficient quantities, may present health risks to
persons who are exposed to it over time. Levels of radon that exceed federal and state
guidelines have been found in buildings in Florida. Additional information regarding
radon and radon testing may be obtained from your county health department.

30. Severability. If any provision of this Lease is invalid or unenforceable under
applicable law, that provision is ineffective only to the extent of the invalidity, and
the remaining provisions of this Lease remain in full force.

31. Governing Law. This Lease is governed by the laws of the State of Florida, including
Part II of Chapter 83, Florida Statutes.
