TOWNHOME LEASE AGREEMENT
Willow Run Townhomes

Willow Run Rentals, LLC ("Landlord") leases to Bastian Kroll and Ngozi Kroll (together, "Tenant") Townhome 31 (the "Townhome") at Willow Run Townhomes, 3300 Willow Run Circle, Tallahassee, Florida 32308, a community of 48 attached townhomes owned and rented by Landlord. The term is twelve months, from January 1, 2029 through December 31, 2029. Monthly rent is $1,980.00.

ARTICLE I: PAYMENT OF RENT
Rent is payable in advance, without demand, on or before the first day of each month through the resident portal or by money order or cashier's check delivered to the leasing office. Landlord may require certified funds after any returned payment. Partial payments may be accepted without waiving Landlord's rights, as permitted by law.

ARTICLE II: LATE CHARGES
If any rent is not received by 11:59 p.m. on the third day of the month, Tenant will pay an initial late charge of $75.00 plus $10.00 per day thereafter until paid, not to exceed $150.00 in total for any month. A $50.00 fee applies to any returned payment.

ARTICLE III: SECURITY DEPOSIT
Tenant has paid a security deposit of $1,980.00, held in a separate non-interest-bearing account at Capital City Trust Bank, 301 S. Monroe Street, Tallahassee, Florida 32301. Tenant is not entitled to interest. Landlord will return the deposit within 15 days after the Lease ends if it makes no claim and will give written notice of any claim within 30 days after the Lease ends.

ARTICLE IV: CONDITION AND REPAIRS
Tenant has inspected the Townhome and accepts it as is. Except as expressly provided in this Lease, Landlord will not be required to make any repairs to the Townhome.

ARTICLE V: OCCUPANTS
Only the persons named in this Lease as Tenant or as authorized occupants may live in the Townhome. Any other person who stays in the Townhome for more than the number of nights allowed in the Community Policies must be approved in writing by Landlord and may be required to complete a rental application. Landlord may require a person who has stayed beyond that period to be added to this Lease as a party.

ARTICLE VI: USE OF THE PREMISES
The Townhome may be used only as a private residence. Tenant may not operate a business in the Townhome that involves customers, clients or employees coming to the Townhome, inventory storage, or signage. Working from home with a computer and telephone is permitted. Tenant may not use the Townhome for any purpose that violates federal, state or local law.

ARTICLE VII: CONDUCT
Tenant, occupants and guests must not engage in conduct that disturbs other residents' peaceful enjoyment of the community, including loud music, shouting, or activity that causes excessive vibration. Quiet hours are from 10:00 p.m. to 8:00 a.m. Sunday through Thursday and from 11:00 p.m. to 9:00 a.m. on Friday and Saturday nights. Tenant is responsible for the conduct of occupants and guests.

ARTICLE VIII: SMOKING
Smoking of any substance, including tobacco, cannabis and electronic cigarettes or vaporizers, is prohibited inside the Townhome, on balconies and patios, and within 25 feet of any building entrance. Tenant will be charged for the reasonable cost of removing smoke odor or residue caused by smoking in violation of this section.

ARTICLE IX: PARKING AND VEHICLES
Each household may park up to two registered, operable vehicles in unassigned spaces. Boats, trailers, campers, commercial trucks and inoperable vehicles may not be parked on the property. Vehicles may not be repaired on the property except for emergency repairs such as changing a flat tire. Vehicles parked in fire lanes, handicapped spaces without a permit, or reserved spaces may be towed at the vehicle owner's expense by the towing company posted at the entrance.

ARTICLE X: TRASH AND RECYCLING
Tenant will place all household trash in sealed bags in the compactor or dumpsters provided, and recyclables in the marked containers. Trash may not be left outside the Townhome door, on balconies, or in breezeways. Large items such as furniture and mattresses must be taken off the property by Tenant or placed for bulk pickup on the scheduled day.

ARTICLE XI: POOL AND AMENITIES
The pool, fitness center, clubhouse and other amenities may be used only during posted hours and in accordance with posted rules. Children under 14 must be accompanied by an adult in the pool and fitness areas. Glass containers are not allowed in the pool area. Use of the amenities is a privilege that may be suspended for violation of the rules, and amenity hours may change seasonally.

ARTICLE XII: RENTER'S INSURANCE
Tenant must obtain and keep in force during the Lease term a renter's insurance policy with personal liability coverage of at least $100,000 and must provide proof of coverage before move-in and upon request. Landlord's property insurance covers the building, not Tenant's furniture, clothing, electronics or other belongings, and does not provide liability coverage for Tenant.

ARTICLE XIII: MOISTURE AND MOLD
Florida's climate requires steady air conditioning and ventilation to control humidity. Tenant will keep the air conditioning running at a setting no higher than 80 degrees, use exhaust fans while bathing and cooking, wipe up standing water promptly, and report any visible mold, water leaks, or musty odors to Landlord in writing within 24 hours of discovering them.

ARTICLE XIV: PEST CONTROL
Landlord provides routine pest control treatment for common household insects. Tenant will keep the Townhome clean and free of food debris and clutter that attracts pests and will prepare the Townhome for scheduled treatments as instructed. Tenant must report any sign of bed bugs immediately and must not attempt to treat bed bugs with over-the-counter products.

ARTICLE XV: SMOKE DETECTORS
The Townhome is equipped with smoke detectors. Tenant will test the detectors monthly, will not disconnect or remove them or their batteries, and will promptly report any detector that does not work.

ARTICLE XVI: ALTERATIONS
Tenant may not paint, wallpaper, install fixtures, replace appliances, or make other alterations to the Townhome without Landlord's prior written consent. Small nails for hanging pictures are permitted. Any alteration made with consent becomes part of the Townhome unless Landlord agrees otherwise in writing.

ARTICLE XVII: APPLIANCES
The Townhome includes a refrigerator, range, dishwasher, microwave and washer and dryer connections. Tenant will use the appliances only for their intended purposes and in accordance with the manufacturers' instructions. Tenant may not install additional major appliances, such as a second refrigerator or a portable dishwasher, without Landlord's written consent.

ARTICLE XVIII: AIR CONDITIONING FILTERS
Tenant will replace the air conditioning filter at least every 60 days with a filter of the size and type specified by Landlord, and will keep the return air vent unobstructed.

ARTICLE XIX: REPAIR REQUESTS
Tenant must make all repair requests in writing through the resident portal or by written notice delivered to the office, except in an emergency involving fire, flooding, gas, or a lack of air conditioning in extreme heat, which Tenant should also report by telephone to the emergency maintenance line.

ARTICLE XX: DAMAGE CAUSED BY RESIDENTS
Tenant will pay Landlord for the reasonable cost of repairing any damage to the Townhome or the community, including stopped-up drains and broken windows, that is caused by Tenant, occupants, guests or animals, whether by negligence, misuse or violation of this Lease. Normal wear and tear is excepted.

ARTICLE XXI: HURRICANES AND SEVERE WEATHER
When a hurricane watch or warning is issued for the area, Tenant will bring in all patio and balcony furniture, grills, plants and other loose items. Tenant must follow any evacuation order issued by local authorities. Tenant may not install storm shutters or tape windows without Landlord's consent.

ARTICLE XXII: UTILITIES
Tenant will place electric and internet service in Tenant's name. Water, sewer and trash service are furnished by Landlord and billed back monthly. Landlord may suspend utility services furnished by Landlord only as permitted by applicable law.

ARTICLE XXIII: ENTRY
Landlord may enter the Townhome to make repairs or inspect it after giving at least 24 hours' notice, between 8:00 a.m. and 8:00 p.m., and at any time in an emergency.

ARTICLE XXIV: ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING
Tenant may not assign this Lease or sublet any part of the Townhome, including through short-term rental platforms, without Landlord's prior written consent.

ARTICLE XXV: KEYS AND ACCESS DEVICES
Tenant will receive two keys to the Townhome, one mailbox key, and one access fob for each adult resident. Replacement fobs cost $25 each. All keys and devices must be returned at move-out.

ARTICLE XXVI: ANIMALS
No animals of any kind, including visiting animals, are allowed in the Townhome without a signed animal addendum, except assistance animals approved as required by law. Approved pets require a nonrefundable pet fee of $350 and monthly pet rent of $35 per animal.

ARTICLE XXVII: GUESTS
Guests may not stay in the Townhome more than 7 consecutive nights or more than 14 nights in any six-month period without Landlord's written consent.

ARTICLE XXVIII: DEFAULT
If rent is not paid when due, Landlord may deliver a written demand for payment or possession giving Tenant 3 days, excluding Saturday, Sunday, and legal holidays.

ARTICLE XXIX: NOTICE TO VACATE
Tenant must give Landlord written notice of intent to vacate at least seventy-five (75) days before the end of the Lease term.

ARTICLE XXX: ATTORNEY FEES
The prevailing party in any action arising under this Lease may recover reasonable attorney fees; however, no attorney fees may be recovered by either party in any claim for personal injury.

ARTICLE XXXI: JURY TRIAL
To the extent permitted by law, Landlord and Tenant waive trial by jury in any lawsuit arising out of this Lease or Tenant's occupancy of the Townhome.

ARTICLE XXXII: NOTICES
Notices to Landlord: Willow Run Rentals, LLC, 3300 Willow Run Circle, Office, Tallahassee, Florida 32308.

ARTICLE XXXIII: RADON GAS
RADON GAS: Radon is a naturally occurring radioactive gas that, when it has accumulated in a building in sufficient quantities, may present health risks to persons who are exposed to it over time. Levels of radon that exceed federal and state guidelines have been found in buildings in Florida. Additional information regarding radon and radon testing may be obtained from your county health department.

ARTICLE XXXIV: MOVE-OUT CONDITION
At move-out, Tenant must leave the Townhome clean, with all trash removed, and in the same condition as at move-in except for normal wear and tear. Tenant should schedule a move-out inspection with the office.

ARTICLE XXXV: SEVERABILITY
If any provision of this Lease is invalid or unenforceable under applicable law, that provision is ineffective only to the extent of the invalidity, and the remaining provisions of this Lease remain in full force.

ARTICLE XXXVI: ENTIRE AGREEMENT
This Lease, its addenda and the Community Policies are the entire agreement between Landlord and Tenant. No oral promises or representations by Landlord's employees or agents are binding unless written into this Lease or an addendum signed by both parties.

ARTICLE XXXVII: GOVERNING LAW
This Lease is governed by the laws of the State of Florida, including Part II of Chapter 83, Florida Statutes.
