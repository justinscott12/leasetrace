RESIDENTIAL LEASE

Bellweather Properties, Inc. ("Landlord") leases to Imani Okonkwo ("Tenant") Unit 4 (the "Unit") of the eight-unit apartment building at 1907 Fern Street, West Palm Beach, Florida 33401, for a term of twelve months beginning August 1, 2027 and ending July 31, 2028, at a monthly rent of $1,520.00.

ARTICLE I: PAYMENT OF RENT
Rent is payable in advance, without demand, on or before the first day of each month through the resident portal or by money order or cashier's check delivered to the leasing office. Landlord may require certified funds after any returned payment. Partial payments may be accepted without waiving Landlord's rights, as permitted by law.

ARTICLE II: LATE CHARGES
If any rent is not received by 11:59 p.m. on the third day of the month, Tenant will pay an initial late charge of $75.00 plus $10.00 per day thereafter until paid, not to exceed $150.00 in total for any month. A $50.00 fee applies to any returned payment.

ARTICLE III: SECURITY DEPOSIT
Tenant has paid a security deposit of $1,520.00, which Landlord holds in a separate interest-bearing account at Palm Coast Savings, 120 Clematis Street, West Palm Beach, Florida 33401. Tenant will receive simple interest on the deposit at 5 percent per year.

ARTICLE IV: CONDITION OF UNIT
Tenant has inspected the Unit and accepts it in its present, AS-IS condition. Landlord has no obligation to make repairs, replacements or improvements to the Unit or the building except those Landlord elects to make in its sole discretion.

ARTICLE V: OCCUPANTS
Only the persons named in this Lease as Tenant or as authorized occupants may live in the Unit. Any other person who stays in the Unit for more than the number of nights allowed in the Community Policies must be approved in writing by Landlord and may be required to complete a rental application. Landlord may require a person who has stayed beyond that period to be added to this Lease as a party.

ARTICLE VI: USE OF THE PREMISES
The Unit may be used only as a private residence. Tenant may not operate a business in the Unit that involves customers, clients or employees coming to the Unit, inventory storage, or signage. Working from home with a computer and telephone is permitted. Tenant may not use the Unit for any purpose that violates federal, state or local law.

ARTICLE VII: CONDUCT
Tenant, occupants and guests must not engage in conduct that disturbs other residents' peaceful enjoyment of the community, including loud music, shouting, or activity that causes excessive vibration. Quiet hours are from 10:00 p.m. to 8:00 a.m. Sunday through Thursday and from 11:00 p.m. to 9:00 a.m. on Friday and Saturday nights. Tenant is responsible for the conduct of occupants and guests.

ARTICLE VIII: SMOKING
Smoking of any substance, including tobacco, cannabis and electronic cigarettes or vaporizers, is prohibited inside the Unit, on balconies and patios, and within 25 feet of any building entrance. Tenant will be charged for the reasonable cost of removing smoke odor or residue caused by smoking in violation of this section.

ARTICLE IX: PARKING AND VEHICLES
Each household may park up to two registered, operable vehicles in unassigned spaces. Boats, trailers, campers, commercial trucks and inoperable vehicles may not be parked on the property. Vehicles may not be repaired on the property except for emergency repairs such as changing a flat tire. Vehicles parked in fire lanes, handicapped spaces without a permit, or reserved spaces may be towed at the vehicle owner's expense by the towing company posted at the entrance.

ARTICLE X: TRASH AND RECYCLING
Tenant will place all household trash in sealed bags in the compactor or dumpsters provided, and recyclables in the marked containers. Trash may not be left outside the Unit door, on balconies, or in breezeways. Large items such as furniture and mattresses must be taken off the property by Tenant or placed for bulk pickup on the scheduled day.

ARTICLE XI: RENTER'S INSURANCE
Tenant must obtain and keep in force during the Lease term a renter's insurance policy with personal liability coverage of at least $100,000 and must provide proof of coverage before move-in and upon request. Landlord's property insurance covers the building, not Tenant's furniture, clothing, electronics or other belongings, and does not provide liability coverage for Tenant.

ARTICLE XII: MOISTURE AND MOLD
Florida's climate requires steady air conditioning and ventilation to control humidity. Tenant will keep the air conditioning running at a setting no higher than 80 degrees, use exhaust fans while bathing and cooking, wipe up standing water promptly, and report any visible mold, water leaks, or musty odors to Landlord in writing within 24 hours of discovering them.

ARTICLE XIII: PEST CONTROL
Landlord provides routine pest control treatment for common household insects. Tenant will keep the Unit clean and free of food debris and clutter that attracts pests and will prepare the Unit for scheduled treatments as instructed. Tenant must report any sign of bed bugs immediately and must not attempt to treat bed bugs with over-the-counter products.

ARTICLE XIV: SMOKE DETECTORS
The Unit is equipped with smoke detectors. Tenant will test the detectors monthly, will not disconnect or remove them or their batteries, and will promptly report any detector that does not work.

ARTICLE XV: ALTERATIONS
Tenant may not paint, wallpaper, install fixtures, replace appliances, or make other alterations to the Unit without Landlord's prior written consent. Small nails for hanging pictures are permitted. Any alteration made with consent becomes part of the Unit unless Landlord agrees otherwise in writing.

ARTICLE XVI: APPLIANCES
The Unit includes a refrigerator, range, dishwasher, microwave and washer and dryer connections. Tenant will use the appliances only for their intended purposes and in accordance with the manufacturers' instructions. Tenant may not install additional major appliances, such as a second refrigerator or a portable dishwasher, without Landlord's written consent.

ARTICLE XVII: AIR CONDITIONING FILTERS
Tenant will replace the air conditioning filter at least every 60 days with a filter of the size and type specified by Landlord, and will keep the return air vent unobstructed.

ARTICLE XVIII: REPAIR REQUESTS
Tenant must make all repair requests in writing through the resident portal or by written notice delivered to the office, except in an emergency involving fire, flooding, gas, or a lack of air conditioning in extreme heat, which Tenant should also report by telephone to the emergency maintenance line.

ARTICLE XIX: DAMAGE CAUSED BY RESIDENTS
Tenant will pay Landlord for the reasonable cost of repairing any damage to the Unit or the community, including stopped-up drains and broken windows, that is caused by Tenant, occupants, guests or animals, whether by negligence, misuse or violation of this Lease. Normal wear and tear is excepted.

ARTICLE XX: HURRICANES AND SEVERE WEATHER
When a hurricane watch or warning is issued for the area, Tenant will bring in all patio and balcony furniture, grills, plants and other loose items. Tenant must follow any evacuation order issued by local authorities. Tenant may not install storm shutters or tape windows without Landlord's consent.

ARTICLE XXI: ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING
Tenant may not assign this Lease or sublet any part of the Unit, including through short-term rental platforms, without Landlord's prior written consent.

ARTICLE XXII: KEYS AND ACCESS DEVICES
Tenant will receive two keys to the Unit, one mailbox key, and one access fob for each adult resident. Replacement fobs cost $25 each. All keys and devices must be returned at move-out.

ARTICLE XXIII: MOVE-IN INSPECTION
Before move-in, Tenant will receive an inspection form. Tenant should note on the form any existing damage or defects and return it to Landlord within 48 hours after move-in. The form is not a request for repairs; repair requests must be made separately in writing or through the resident portal.

ARTICLE XXIV: ANIMALS
No animals of any kind, including visiting animals, are allowed in the Unit without a signed animal addendum, except assistance animals approved as required by law. Approved pets require a nonrefundable pet fee of $350 and monthly pet rent of $35 per animal.

ARTICLE XXV: GUESTS
Guests may not stay in the Unit more than 7 consecutive nights or more than 14 nights in any six-month period without Landlord's written consent.

ARTICLE XXVI: ENTRY
Landlord may enter the Unit with at least twenty-four (24) hours' notice, between 8:00 a.m. and 7:00 p.m., to inspect, make repairs, or show the Unit, and at any time in an emergency.

ARTICLE XXVII: DEFAULT
If Tenant fails to pay rent when due, Landlord may give Tenant a written three-day notice, excluding Saturday, Sunday, and legal holidays, demanding payment or possession.

ARTICLE XXVIII: NON-RENEWAL
Either party who does not intend to renew must give the other written notice at least thirty (30) days before the end of the term.

ARTICLE XXIX: HOLDOVER
If Tenant remains in the Unit after the Lease term with Landlord's consent, the tenancy will be month-to-month at the rent then in effect, and either party may end it by giving the other at least fifteen (15) days' written notice.

ARTICLE XXX: MOVE-OUT CONDITION
At move-out, Tenant must leave the Unit clean, with all trash removed, and in the same condition as at move-in except for normal wear and tear. Tenant should schedule a move-out inspection with the office.

ARTICLE XXXI: RETURN OF DEPOSIT
Within forty-five (45) days after Tenant vacates, Landlord will return the security deposit, less any amounts Landlord is entitled to deduct, together with an itemized statement of any deductions.

ARTICLE XXXII: ATTORNEY FEES
The prevailing party in any action to enforce this Lease may recover reasonable attorney fees and costs.

ARTICLE XXXIII: NOTICES
Notices to Landlord shall be sent to Bellweather Properties, Inc., 2200 N. Flagler Drive, Suite 4, West Palm Beach, Florida 33407.

ARTICLE XXXIV: RADON GAS
RADON GAS: Radon is a naturally occurring radioactive gas that, when it has accumulated in a building in sufficient quantities, may present health risks to persons who are exposed to it over time. Levels of radon that exceed federal and state guidelines have been found in buildings in Florida. Additional information regarding radon and radon testing may be obtained from your county health department.

ARTICLE XXXV: LEAD-BASED PAINT
If the building was constructed before 1978, Tenant acknowledges receipt of the federally required lead-based paint disclosure and the pamphlet Protect Your Family From Lead in Your Home.

ARTICLE XXXVI: ELECTRONIC NOTICES
Tenant consents to receive notices from Landlord by e-mail at the address shown on Tenant's rental application, as permitted by section 83.505, Florida Statutes. Tenant may withdraw this consent at any time by written notice to Landlord.

ARTICLE XXXVII: SEVERABILITY
If any provision of this Lease is invalid or unenforceable under applicable law, that provision is ineffective only to the extent of the invalidity, and the remaining provisions of this Lease remain in full force.

ARTICLE XXXVIII: ENTIRE AGREEMENT
This Lease, its addenda and the Community Policies are the entire agreement between Landlord and Tenant. No oral promises or representations by Landlord's employees or agents are binding unless written into this Lease or an addendum signed by both parties.

ARTICLE XXXIX: GOVERNING LAW
This Lease is governed by the laws of the State of Florida, including Part II of Chapter 83, Florida Statutes.
