APARTMENT LEASE AGREEMENT
Cypress Hollow Apartments

This Lease is made on April 3, 2027 between Cypress Hollow Owner, LLC, acting through its managing agent Larkspur Residential (together, "Owner"), and Tamsin Greer and Luis Ocampo (together, "Resident"). Owner leases to Resident Apartment 1123 (the "Apartment") at Cypress Hollow Apartments, 5600 Old Cheney Highway, Orlando, Florida 32807, a 312-unit apartment community, for a term beginning May 1, 2027 and ending April 30, 2028. Monthly rent is $1,795.00.

1. Payment of Rent. Rent is payable in advance, without demand, on or before the first day of each month through the resident portal or by money order or cashier's check delivered to the leasing office. Owner may require certified funds after any returned payment. Partial payments may be accepted without waiving Owner's rights, as permitted by law.

2. Late Charges. If any rent is not received by 11:59 p.m. on the third day of the month, Resident will pay an initial late charge of $75.00 plus $10.00 per day thereafter until paid, not to exceed $150.00 in total for any month. A $50.00 fee applies to any returned payment.

3. Security Deposit. Resident has paid a security deposit of $1,000.00. Owner will hold the deposit in a separate non-interest-bearing account at Seminole River Bank, 300 S. Orange Avenue, Orlando, Florida 32801. Resident is not entitled to interest on the deposit.

4. Occupants. Only the persons named in this Lease as Resident or as authorized occupants may live in the Apartment. Any other person who stays in the Apartment for more than the number of nights allowed in the Community Policies must be approved in writing by Owner and may be required to complete a rental application. Owner may require a person who has stayed beyond that period to be added to this Lease as a party.

5. Use of the Premises. The Apartment may be used only as a private residence. Resident may not operate a business in the Apartment that involves customers, clients or employees coming to the Apartment, inventory storage, or signage. Working from home with a computer and telephone is permitted. Resident may not use the Apartment for any purpose that violates federal, state or local law.

6. Guests. Guests may not stay in the Apartment more than 7 consecutive nights or more than 14 nights in any six-month period without Owner's written consent.

7. Conduct. Resident, occupants and guests must not engage in conduct that disturbs other residents' peaceful enjoyment of the community, including loud music, shouting, or activity that causes excessive vibration. Quiet hours are from 10:00 p.m. to 8:00 a.m. Sunday through Thursday and from 11:00 p.m. to 9:00 a.m. on Friday and Saturday nights. Resident is responsible for the conduct of occupants and guests.

8. Smoking. Smoking of any substance, including tobacco, cannabis and electronic cigarettes or vaporizers, is prohibited inside the Apartment, on balconies and patios, and within 25 feet of any building entrance. Resident will be charged for the reasonable cost of removing smoke odor or residue caused by smoking in violation of this section.

9. Parking and Vehicles. Each household may park up to two registered, operable vehicles in unassigned spaces. Boats, trailers, campers, commercial trucks and inoperable vehicles may not be parked on the property. Vehicles may not be repaired on the property except for emergency repairs such as changing a flat tire. Vehicles parked in fire lanes, handicapped spaces without a permit, or reserved spaces may be towed at the vehicle owner's expense by the towing company posted at the entrance.

10. Trash and Recycling. Resident will place all household trash in sealed bags in the compactor or dumpsters provided, and recyclables in the marked containers. Trash may not be left outside the Apartment door, on balconies, or in breezeways. Large items such as furniture and mattresses must be taken off the property by Resident or placed for bulk pickup on the scheduled day.

11. Pool and Amenities. The pool, fitness center, clubhouse and other amenities may be used only during posted hours and in accordance with posted rules. Children under 14 must be accompanied by an adult in the pool and fitness areas. Glass containers are not allowed in the pool area. Use of the amenities is a privilege that may be suspended for violation of the rules, and amenity hours may change seasonally.

12. Renter's Insurance. Resident must obtain and keep in force during the Lease term a renter's insurance policy with personal liability coverage of at least $100,000 and must provide proof of coverage before move-in and upon request. Owner's property insurance covers the building, not Resident's furniture, clothing, electronics or other belongings, and does not provide liability coverage for Resident.

13. Limitation of Liability. Unless otherwise required by law, Owner is not liable to Resident, occupants or guests for any personal injury or for any damage to or loss of personal property, from any cause, including fire, smoke, rain, flood, water leaks, hail, lightning, wind, explosions, theft, or the acts of other residents.

14. Security. Owner does not provide and has no duty to provide security guards or patrol services. Any gates, cameras or lighting are provided for convenience and may not work at all times. Resident should call 911 in any emergency and should take steps to protect Resident's own safety and property.

15. Lockout Service. If Resident is locked out of the Apartment, Resident may call the office during business hours, or the after-hours courtesy line, and a staff member will let Resident in after verifying identity. A $50.00 service charge applies to after-hours lockouts.

16. Access by Owner. Owner and Owner's contractors may enter the Apartment for the purposes listed below after giving Resident at least 24 hours' notice, and only between 7:30 a.m. and 8:00 p.m., except in an emergency or to protect or preserve the premises. The permitted purposes are: (a) to make repairs or perform maintenance; (b) to show the Apartment to prospective residents, lenders or buyers; (c) to perform pest control; (d) to replace air conditioning filters and test smoke detectors; (e) to remove window coverings, decorations or other items that violate the Community Policies; and (f) to interrupt water or electrical service when needed to complete repairs.

17. Moisture and Mold. Florida's climate requires steady air conditioning and ventilation to control humidity. Resident will keep the air conditioning running at a setting no higher than 80 degrees, use exhaust fans while bathing and cooking, wipe up standing water promptly, and report any visible mold, water leaks, or musty odors to Owner in writing within 24 hours of discovering them.

18. Pest Control. Owner provides routine pest control treatment for common household insects. Resident will keep the Apartment clean and free of food debris and clutter that attracts pests and will prepare the Apartment for scheduled treatments as instructed. Resident must report any sign of bed bugs immediately and must not attempt to treat bed bugs with over-the-counter products.

19. Smoke Detectors. The Apartment is equipped with smoke detectors. Resident will test the detectors monthly, will not disconnect or remove them or their batteries, and will promptly report any detector that does not work.

20. Alterations. Resident may not paint, wallpaper, install fixtures, replace appliances, or make other alterations to the Apartment without Owner's prior written consent. Small nails for hanging pictures are permitted. Any alteration made with consent becomes part of the Apartment unless Owner agrees otherwise in writing.

21. Appliances. The Apartment includes a refrigerator, range, dishwasher, microwave and washer and dryer connections. Resident will use the appliances only for their intended purposes and in accordance with the manufacturers' instructions. Resident may not install additional major appliances, such as a second refrigerator or a portable dishwasher, without Owner's written consent.

22. Air Conditioning Filters. Resident will replace the air conditioning filter at least every 60 days with a filter of the size and type specified by Owner, and will keep the return air vent unobstructed.

23. Damage Caused by Residents. Resident will pay Owner for the reasonable cost of repairing any damage to the Apartment or the community, including stopped-up drains and broken windows, that is caused by Resident, occupants, guests or animals, whether by negligence, misuse or violation of this Lease. Normal wear and tear is excepted.

24. Repairs. Owner will comply with applicable building, housing and health codes and will maintain the roof, windows, exterior walls, foundation, other structural components and plumbing in reasonable repair. Owner will make other repairs within a reasonable time after Resident's written request.

25. Hurricanes and Severe Weather. When a hurricane watch or warning is issued for the area, Resident will bring in all patio and balcony furniture, grills, plants and other loose items. Resident must follow any evacuation order issued by local authorities. Resident may not install storm shutters or tape windows without Owner's consent.

26. Packages. The community uses electronic package lockers. Packages that do not fit in a locker will be held at the leasing office for up to seven days. Perishable deliveries must be picked up the same day.

27. Keys and Access Devices. Resident will receive two keys to the Apartment, one mailbox key, and one access fob for each adult resident. Replacement fobs cost $25 each. All keys and devices must be returned at move-out.

28. Satellite Dishes and Antennas. Resident may install one satellite dish or antenna no larger than one meter in diameter entirely within the Apartment or on a balcony or patio under Resident's exclusive control, as permitted by federal law. Installation may not penetrate exterior walls, railings or the roof.

29. Grills and Open Flames. Charcoal and gas grills may not be used or stored on balconies, patios or within 10 feet of any building, as required by the fire code. Electric grills approved by the manufacturer for balcony use are permitted. Candles must never be left unattended.

30. Window Coverings. Window coverings visible from outside must be white or off-white blinds, shades or curtains with white backing. Sheets, towels, foil and other temporary coverings may not be used as window coverings.

31. Animals. No animals of any kind, including visiting animals, are allowed in the Apartment without a signed animal addendum, except assistance animals approved as required by law. Approved pets require a nonrefundable pet fee of $350 and monthly pet rent of $35 per animal.

32. Default by Resident. If Resident fails to pay rent when due, Owner may deliver a written notice demanding payment of the rent or possession of the Apartment within 3 days, excluding Saturday, Sunday, and legal holidays. Owner may recover possession of the Apartment only through a court eviction.

33. Notice of Non-Renewal. If either party does not intend to renew this Lease, that party must give the other written notice at least 60 days before the end of the Lease term. If neither party gives notice, this Lease will continue month-to-month, and either party may end the month-to-month tenancy by written notice given at least 30 days before the end of a monthly period.

34. Deposit Accounting. Within 15 days after Resident surrenders the Apartment, Owner will return the deposit if Owner makes no claim on it. If Owner intends to impose a claim, Owner will send written notice of the claim by certified mail or e-mail within 30 days after Resident surrenders the Apartment.

35. Property Left Behind. If Resident surrenders or abandons the Apartment, Owner may remove and dispose of personal property Resident leaves behind.

BY SIGNING THIS RENTAL AGREEMENT, THE TENANT AGREES THAT UPON SURRENDER, ABANDONMENT, OR RECOVERY OF POSSESSION OF THE DWELLING UNIT DUE TO THE DEATH OF THE LAST REMAINING TENANT, AS PROVIDED BY CHAPTER 83, FLORIDA STATUTES, THE LANDLORD SHALL NOT BE LIABLE OR RESPONSIBLE FOR STORAGE OR DISPOSITION OF THE TENANT'S PERSONAL PROPERTY.

36. Jury Trial. To the extent permitted by law, Owner and Resident waive trial by jury in any lawsuit arising out of this Lease or Resident's occupancy of the Apartment.

37. Assignment and Subletting. Resident may not assign this Lease or sublet any part of the Apartment, including through short-term rental platforms, without Owner's prior written consent.

38. Community Policies. Resident will follow the written Community Policies, which are attached and made part of this Lease. Owner may adopt reasonable changes to the Community Policies that apply to all residents, effective 30 days after written notice to Resident, provided that no change reduces the rent or materially changes the terms of this Lease.

39. Joint and Several Obligations. Each person who signs this Lease as Resident is jointly and severally responsible for all obligations under this Lease, including the full rent, regardless of whether that person continues to live in the Apartment.

40. Electronic Notices. Resident consents to receive notices from Owner by e-mail at the address shown on Resident's rental application, as permitted by section 83.505, Florida Statutes. Resident may withdraw this consent at any time by written notice to Owner.

41. Attorney Fees. In any lawsuit concerning this Lease or the Apartment, the prevailing party may recover reasonable attorney fees and court costs from the nonprevailing party.

42. Notices to Owner. Notices to Owner must be delivered to Larkspur Residential, Cypress Hollow Leasing Office, 5600 Old Cheney Highway, Orlando, Florida 32807.

43. Radon Gas. RADON GAS: Radon is a naturally occurring radioactive gas that, when it has accumulated in a building in sufficient quantities, may present health risks to persons who are exposed to it over time. Levels of radon that exceed federal and state guidelines have been found in buildings in Florida. Additional information regarding radon and radon testing may be obtained from your county health department.

44. Severability. If any provision of this Lease is invalid or unenforceable under applicable law, that provision is ineffective only to the extent of the invalidity, and the remaining provisions of this Lease remain in full force.

45. Entire Agreement. This Lease, its addenda and the Community Policies are the entire agreement between Owner and Resident. No oral promises or representations by Owner's employees or agents are binding unless written into this Lease or an addendum signed by both parties.

46. Governing Law. This Lease is governed by the laws of the State of Florida, including Part II of Chapter 83, Florida Statutes.
