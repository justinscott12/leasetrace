LEASE AGREEMENT - HARBORSIDE APARTMENTS

Lessor: Harborside Pensacola LP ("Lessor"). Lessee: Desmond Achebe ("Lessee").
Apartment: No. 507, Harborside Apartments, 44 Palafox Wharf Road, Pensacola, Florida
32502, in a 96-unit apartment building (the "Apartment"). Term: March 1, 2028 through
February 28, 2029. Rent: $1,640.00 per month.

1. Payment of Rent. Rent is payable in advance, without demand, on or before the first
day of each month through the resident portal or by money order or cashier's check
delivered to the leasing office. Lessor may require certified funds after any returned
payment. Partial payments may be accepted without waiving Lessor's rights, as permitted
by law.

2. Security Deposit. Lessee has paid a security deposit of $1,400.00. The deposit may be
held in any account maintained by Lessor, including Lessor's operating account, at Gulf
Breeze Bank, 18 Garden Street, Pensacola, Florida 32502. Lessee will not receive
interest on the deposit. If Lessor makes no claim, the deposit will be returned within
15 days after the Lease ends; notice of any claim will be sent within 30 days after the
Lease ends.

3. Late Charges. If any rent is not received by 11:59 p.m. on the third day of the
month, Lessee will pay an initial late charge of $75.00 plus $10.00 per day thereafter
until paid, not to exceed $150.00 in total for any month. A $50.00 fee applies to any
returned payment.

4. Rent Default. If rent is not paid when due, Lessor may terminate this Lease after
delivering a written demand for payment or possession and allowing Lessee three (3)
calendar days, including weekends and holidays, to comply.

5. Occupants. Only the persons named in this Lease as Lessee or as authorized occupants
may live in the Apartment. Any other person who stays in the Apartment for more than the
number of nights allowed in the Community Policies must be approved in writing by Lessor
and may be required to complete a rental application. Lessor may require a person who
has stayed beyond that period to be added to this Lease as a party.

6. Use of the Premises. The Apartment may be used only as a private residence. Lessee
may not operate a business in the Apartment that involves customers, clients or
employees coming to the Apartment, inventory storage, or signage. Working from home with
a computer and telephone is permitted. Lessee may not use the Apartment for any purpose
that violates federal, state or local law.

7. Conduct. Lessee, occupants and guests must not engage in conduct that disturbs other
residents' peaceful enjoyment of the community, including loud music, shouting, or
activity that causes excessive vibration. Quiet hours are from 10:00 p.m. to 8:00 a.m.
Sunday through Thursday and from 11:00 p.m. to 9:00 a.m. on Friday and Saturday nights.
Lessee is responsible for the conduct of occupants and guests.

8. Smoking. Smoking of any substance, including tobacco, cannabis and electronic
cigarettes or vaporizers, is prohibited inside the Apartment, on balconies and patios,
and within 25 feet of any building entrance. Lessee will be charged for the reasonable
cost of removing smoke odor or residue caused by smoking in violation of this section.

9. Parking and Vehicles. Each household may park up to two registered, operable vehicles
in unassigned spaces. Boats, trailers, campers, commercial trucks and inoperable
vehicles may not be parked on the property. Vehicles may not be repaired on the property
except for emergency repairs such as changing a flat tire. Vehicles parked in fire
lanes, handicapped spaces without a permit, or reserved spaces may be towed at the
vehicle owner's expense by the towing company posted at the entrance.

10. Trash and Recycling. Lessee will place all household trash in sealed bags in the
compactor or dumpsters provided, and recyclables in the marked containers. Trash may not
be left outside the Apartment door, on balconies, or in breezeways. Large items such as
furniture and mattresses must be taken off the property by Lessee or placed for bulk
pickup on the scheduled day.

11. Pool and Amenities. The pool, fitness center, clubhouse and other amenities may be
used only during posted hours and in accordance with posted rules. Children under 14
must be accompanied by an adult in the pool and fitness areas. Glass containers are not
allowed in the pool area. Use of the amenities is a privilege that may be suspended for
violation of the rules, and amenity hours may change seasonally.

12. Renter's Insurance. Lessee must obtain and keep in force during the Lease term a
renter's insurance policy with personal liability coverage of at least $100,000 and must
provide proof of coverage before move-in and upon request. Lessor's property insurance
covers the building, not Lessee's furniture, clothing, electronics or other belongings,
and does not provide liability coverage for Lessee.

13. Moisture and Mold. Florida's climate requires steady air conditioning and
ventilation to control humidity. Lessee will keep the air conditioning running at a
setting no higher than 80 degrees, use exhaust fans while bathing and cooking, wipe up
standing water promptly, and report any visible mold, water leaks, or musty odors to
Lessor in writing within 24 hours of discovering them.

14. Pest Control. Lessor provides routine pest control treatment for common household
insects. Lessee will keep the Apartment clean and free of food debris and clutter that
attracts pests and will prepare the Apartment for scheduled treatments as instructed.
Lessee must report any sign of bed bugs immediately and must not attempt to treat bed
bugs with over-the-counter products.

15. Smoke Detectors. The Apartment is equipped with smoke detectors. Lessee will test
the detectors monthly, will not disconnect or remove them or their batteries, and will
promptly report any detector that does not work.

16. Alterations. Lessee may not paint, wallpaper, install fixtures, replace appliances,
or make other alterations to the Apartment without Lessor's prior written consent. Small
nails for hanging pictures are permitted. Any alteration made with consent becomes part
of the Apartment unless Lessor agrees otherwise in writing.

17. Appliances. The Apartment includes a refrigerator, range, dishwasher, microwave and
washer and dryer connections. Lessee will use the appliances only for their intended
purposes and in accordance with the manufacturers' instructions. Lessee may not install
additional major appliances, such as a second refrigerator or a portable dishwasher,
without Lessor's written consent.

18. Balconies and Patios. Nothing may be hung from, attached to, or displayed on any
balcony, patio, railing or window, including flags of any kind, banners, signs, laundry
or towels.

19. Hurricanes and Severe Weather. When a hurricane watch or warning is issued for the
area, Lessee will bring in all patio and balcony furniture, grills, plants and other
loose items. Lessee must follow any evacuation order issued by local authorities. Lessee
may not install storm shutters or tape windows without Lessor's consent.

20. Packages. The community uses electronic package lockers. Packages that do not fit in
a locker will be held at the leasing office for up to seven days. Perishable deliveries
must be picked up the same day.

21. Keys and Access Devices. Lessee will receive two keys to the Apartment, one mailbox
key, and one access fob for each adult resident. Replacement fobs cost $25 each. All
keys and devices must be returned at move-out.

22. Grills and Open Flames. Charcoal and gas grills may not be used or stored on
balconies, patios or within 10 feet of any building, as required by the fire code.
Electric grills approved by the manufacturer for balcony use are permitted. Candles must
never be left unattended.

23. Assignment and Subletting. Lessee may not assign this Lease or sublet any part of
the Apartment, including through short-term rental platforms, without Lessor's prior
written consent.

24. Access. Lessor may enter the Apartment to make repairs or inspect it upon 24 hours'
notice between 8:00 a.m. and 6:00 p.m., and at any time in an emergency.

25. Maintenance. Lessor will keep the building's structure, roof and plumbing in good
repair and comply with applicable housing codes. Lessee will keep the Apartment clean.

26. Damage Caused by Residents. Lessee will pay Lessor for the reasonable cost of
repairing any damage to the Apartment or the community, including stopped-up drains and
broken windows, that is caused by Lessee, occupants, guests or animals, whether by
negligence, misuse or violation of this Lease. Normal wear and tear is excepted.

27. Animals. No animals of any kind, including visiting animals, are allowed in the
Apartment without a signed animal addendum, except assistance animals approved as
required by law. Approved pets require a nonrefundable pet fee of $350 and monthly pet
rent of $35 per animal.

28. Jury Trial. To the extent permitted by law, Lessor and Lessee waive trial by jury in
any lawsuit arising out of this Lease or Lessee's occupancy of the Apartment.

29. End of Term. Lessee will give Lessor written notice at least 30 days before the end
of the term if Lessee will not renew.

30. Abandoned Property. After Lessee surrenders or abandons the Apartment, Lessor may
remove and dispose of any personal property remaining in it, and Lessor will have no
liability for that property.

31. Attorney Fees. The prevailing party in any action under this Lease may recover
reasonable attorney fees.

32. Notices. Notices to Lessor: Harborside Pensacola LP, Management Office, 44 Palafox
Wharf Road, Pensacola, Florida 32502.

33. Radon Gas. RADON GAS: Radon is a naturally occurring radioactive gas that, when it
has accumulated in a building in sufficient quantities, may present health risks to
persons who are exposed to it over time. Levels of radon that exceed federal and state
guidelines have been found in buildings in Florida. Additional information regarding
radon and radon testing may be obtained from your county health department.

34. Severability. If any provision of this Lease is invalid or unenforceable under
applicable law, that provision is ineffective only to the extent of the invalidity, and
the remaining provisions of this Lease remain in full force.

35. Governing Law. This Lease is governed by the laws of the State of Florida, including
Part II of Chapter 83, Florida Statutes.
