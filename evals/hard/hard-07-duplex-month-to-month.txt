MONTH-TO-MONTH RENTAL AGREEMENT

Owner: Rafael and Lucia Montoya ("Owner"). Tenant: Petra Lindqvist ("Tenant"). Residence: the north unit of the duplex at 812 Tamarind Avenue, Fort Myers, Florida 33901 (the "Residence"). This is a month-to-month tenancy beginning February 1, 2029. Rent is $1,350.00 per month.

ENDING THE TENANCY
Either party may end this month-to-month tenancy by written notice delivered on or before the fifteenth (15th) day of a month, effective at the end of that month.

PAYMENT OF RENT
Rent is payable in advance, without demand, on or before the first day of each month through the resident portal or by money order or cashier's check delivered to the leasing office. Owner may require certified funds after any returned payment. Partial payments may be accepted without waiving Owner's rights, as permitted by law.

LATE CHARGES
If any rent is not received by 11:59 p.m. on the third day of the month, Tenant will pay an initial late charge of $75.00 plus $10.00 per day thereafter until paid, not to exceed $150.00 in total for any month. A $50.00 fee applies to any returned payment.

SECURITY DEPOSIT
Tenant has paid a security deposit of $1,350.00, which Owner holds in a separate non-interest-bearing account at Caloosa Community Bank, 2040 First Street, Fort Myers, Florida 33901. Tenant will not receive interest on the deposit. See the Deposit Addendum for return of the deposit.

USE OF THE PREMISES
The Residence may be used only as a private residence. Tenant may not operate a business in the Residence that involves customers, clients or employees coming to the Residence, inventory storage, or signage. Working from home with a computer and telephone is permitted. Tenant may not use the Residence for any purpose that violates federal, state or local law.

SMOKING
Smoking of any substance, including tobacco, cannabis and electronic cigarettes or vaporizers, is prohibited inside the Residence, on balconies and patios, and within 25 feet of any building entrance. Tenant will be charged for the reasonable cost of removing smoke odor or residue caused by smoking in violation of this section.

RENTER'S INSURANCE
Tenant must obtain and keep in force during the Lease term a renter's insurance policy with personal liability coverage of at least $100,000 and must provide proof of coverage before move-in and upon request. Owner's property insurance covers the building, not Tenant's furniture, clothing, electronics or other belongings, and does not provide liability coverage for Tenant.

REPAIRS
Because the Residence is one half of a duplex, Owner and Tenant agree in writing that Tenant will make and pay for repairs to the plumbing fixtures and drains inside the Residence. Owner will maintain the roof, foundation, exterior walls and the plumbing lines outside the Residence.

MOISTURE AND MOLD
Florida's climate requires steady air conditioning and ventilation to control humidity. Tenant will keep the air conditioning running at a setting no higher than 80 degrees, use exhaust fans while bathing and cooking, wipe up standing water promptly, and report any visible mold, water leaks, or musty odors to Owner in writing within 24 hours of discovering them.

PEST CONTROL
Owner provides routine pest control treatment for common household insects. Tenant will keep the Residence clean and free of food debris and clutter that attracts pests and will prepare the Residence for scheduled treatments as instructed. Tenant must report any sign of bed bugs immediately and must not attempt to treat bed bugs with over-the-counter products.

SMOKE DETECTORS
The Residence is equipped with smoke detectors. Tenant will test the detectors monthly, will not disconnect or remove them or their batteries, and will promptly report any detector that does not work.

ALTERATIONS
Tenant may not paint, wallpaper, install fixtures, replace appliances, or make other alterations to the Residence without Owner's prior written consent. Small nails for hanging pictures are permitted. Any alteration made with consent becomes part of the Residence unless Owner agrees otherwise in writing.

AIR CONDITIONING FILTERS
Tenant will replace the air conditioning filter at least every 60 days with a filter of the size and type specified by Owner, and will keep the return air vent unobstructed.

HURRICANES AND SEVERE WEATHER
When a hurricane watch or warning is issued for the area, Tenant will bring in all patio and balcony furniture, grills, plants and other loose items. Tenant must follow any evacuation order issued by local authorities. Tenant may not install storm shutters or tape windows without Owner's consent.

ENTRY
Owner will give Tenant at least 24 hours' notice before entering to make repairs and will enter only between 8:00 a.m. and 8:00 p.m., except in an emergency.

LOCKS
Tenant may not add or change locks without Owner's consent and must give Owner a key to any approved lock.

ANIMALS
No animals of any kind, including visiting animals, are allowed in the Residence without a signed animal addendum, except assistance animals approved as required by law. Approved pets require a nonrefundable pet fee of $350 and monthly pet rent of $35 per animal.

GUESTS
Guests may not stay in the Residence more than 7 consecutive nights or more than 14 nights in any six-month period without Owner's written consent.

UNPAID RENT
If rent is not paid when due, Owner may give Tenant a written notice to pay or vacate within 3 days, excluding Saturday, Sunday, and legal holidays.

ATTORNEY FEES
The prevailing party in any action concerning this Agreement may recover reasonable attorney fees.

RADON GAS
RADON GAS: Radon is a naturally occurring radioactive gas that, when it has accumulated in a building in sufficient quantities, may present health risks to persons who are exposed to it over time. Levels of radon that exceed federal and state guidelines have been found in buildings in Florida. Additional information regarding radon and radon testing may be obtained from your county health department.

GOVERNING LAW
This Lease is governed by the laws of the State of Florida, including Part II of Chapter 83, Florida Statutes.

DEPOSIT ADDENDUM
1. If Owner makes no claim on the deposit, Owner will return it within 15 days after Tenant vacates. 2. If Owner intends to impose a claim on the deposit, Owner will mail written notice of the claim within thirty-five (35) days after Tenant vacates. 3. Tenant must provide a forwarding address in writing.
