SINGLE-FAMILY RESIDENTIAL LEASE

Landlord: Marguerite Ashby ("Landlord"). Tenant: Corwin and Delia Pruitt ("Tenant"). Home: 4417 Sandpiper Lane, Sarasota, Florida 34231, a detached single-family house (the "Home"). Term: one year, from June 1, 2028 through May 31, 2029. Rent: $2,850.00 per month.

PAYMENT OF RENT
Rent is payable in advance, without demand, on or before the first day of each month through the resident portal or by money order or cashier's check delivered to the leasing office. Landlord may require certified funds after any returned payment. Partial payments may be accepted without waiving Landlord's rights, as permitted by law.

LATE CHARGES
If any rent is not received by 11:59 p.m. on the third day of the month, Tenant will pay an initial late charge of $75.00 plus $10.00 per day thereafter until paid, not to exceed $150.00 in total for any month. A $50.00 fee applies to any returned payment.

SECURITY DEPOSIT
Tenant has paid a security deposit of $2,850.00. Instead of holding the deposit in a separate account, Landlord has posted a surety bond with the Clerk of the Circuit Court of Sarasota County as provided by law. Tenant will receive simple interest on the deposit at 5 percent per year. If Landlord makes no claim, Landlord will return the deposit and interest within 15 days after the Lease ends; Landlord will send notice of any claim within 30 days after the Lease ends.

USE OF THE PREMISES
The Home may be used only as a private residence. Tenant may not operate a business in the Home that involves customers, clients or employees coming to the Home, inventory storage, or signage. Working from home with a computer and telephone is permitted. Tenant may not use the Home for any purpose that violates federal, state or local law.

SMOKING
Smoking of any substance, including tobacco, cannabis and electronic cigarettes or vaporizers, is prohibited inside the Home, on balconies and patios, and within 25 feet of any building entrance. Tenant will be charged for the reasonable cost of removing smoke odor or residue caused by smoking in violation of this section.

RENTER'S INSURANCE
Tenant must obtain and keep in force during the Lease term a renter's insurance policy with personal liability coverage of at least $100,000 and must provide proof of coverage before move-in and upon request. Landlord's property insurance covers the building, not Tenant's furniture, clothing, electronics or other belongings, and does not provide liability coverage for Tenant.

MAINTENANCE BY TENANT
Because the Home is a single-family residence, Landlord and Tenant agree in writing that Tenant will maintain and repair the plumbing system, including drains, fixtures and the water heater, at Tenant's expense. Landlord will maintain the roof, foundation and exterior walls. Tenant will mow the lawn and keep the yard free of debris.

MOISTURE AND MOLD
Florida's climate requires steady air conditioning and ventilation to control humidity. Tenant will keep the air conditioning running at a setting no higher than 80 degrees, use exhaust fans while bathing and cooking, wipe up standing water promptly, and report any visible mold, water leaks, or musty odors to Landlord in writing within 24 hours of discovering them.

PEST CONTROL
Landlord provides routine pest control treatment for common household insects. Tenant will keep the Home clean and free of food debris and clutter that attracts pests and will prepare the Home for scheduled treatments as instructed. Tenant must report any sign of bed bugs immediately and must not attempt to treat bed bugs with over-the-counter products.

SMOKE DETECTORS
The Home is equipped with smoke detectors. Tenant will test the detectors monthly, will not disconnect or remove them or their batteries, and will promptly report any detector that does not work.

ALTERATIONS
Tenant may not paint, wallpaper, install fixtures, replace appliances, or make other alterations to the Home without Landlord's prior written consent. Small nails for hanging pictures are permitted. Any alteration made with consent becomes part of the Home unless Landlord agrees otherwise in writing.

APPLIANCES
The Home includes a refrigerator, range, dishwasher, microwave and washer and dryer connections. Tenant will use the appliances only for their intended purposes and in accordance with the manufacturers' instructions. Tenant may not install additional major appliances, such as a second refrigerator or a portable dishwasher, without Landlord's written consent.

AIR CONDITIONING FILTERS
Tenant will replace the air conditioning filter at least every 60 days with a filter of the size and type specified by Landlord, and will keep the return air vent unobstructed.

HURRICANES AND SEVERE WEATHER
When a hurricane watch or warning is issued for the area, Tenant will bring in all patio and balcony furniture, grills, plants and other loose items. Tenant must follow any evacuation order issued by local authorities. Tenant may not install storm shutters or tape windows without Landlord's consent.

UTILITIES FURNISHED BY LANDLORD
Water and sewer service for the Home are billed to Landlord. Tenant will reimburse Landlord for water and sewer charges within 10 days after receiving a copy of each bill. If Tenant does not reimburse those charges when due, Landlord may have water service to the Home discontinued until Tenant pays.

ACCESS
Landlord may enter the Home to inspect it, make repairs, or show it to prospective tenants or buyers upon at least twelve (12) hours' notice to Tenant, between 9:00 a.m. and 6:00 p.m.

REPORTS TO AUTHORITIES
Tenant agrees to give Landlord written notice of any condition in the Home and thirty (30) days to correct it before reporting the condition to any governmental authority. If Tenant reports a condition to a governmental authority before that period ends, Landlord may terminate this Lease on thirty (30) days' notice.

ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING
Tenant may not assign this Lease or sublet any part of the Home, including through short-term rental platforms, without Landlord's prior written consent.

SATELLITE DISHES AND ANTENNAS
Tenant may install one satellite dish or antenna no larger than one meter in diameter entirely within the Home or on a balcony or patio under Tenant's exclusive control, as permitted by federal law. Installation may not penetrate exterior walls, railings or the roof.

ANIMALS
No animals of any kind, including visiting animals, are allowed in the Home without a signed animal addendum, except assistance animals approved as required by law. Approved pets require a nonrefundable pet fee of $350 and monthly pet rent of $35 per animal.

GUESTS
Guests may not stay in the Home more than 7 consecutive nights or more than 14 nights in any six-month period without Landlord's written consent.

DEFAULT
If rent is not paid when due, Landlord may deliver a written notice allowing Tenant three days, excluding Saturday, Sunday, and legal holidays, to pay the rent or deliver possession.

NON-RENEWAL
Either party who does not intend to renew this Lease must give the other written notice at least sixty (60) days before the end of the term.

JURY TRIAL
To the extent permitted by law, Landlord and Tenant waive trial by jury in any lawsuit arising out of this Lease or Tenant's occupancy of the Home.

ATTORNEY FEES
The prevailing party in any lawsuit concerning this Lease may recover reasonable attorney fees and court costs.

NOTICES

RADON GAS: Radon is a naturally occurring radioactive gas that, when it has accumulated in a building in sufficient quantities, may present health risks to persons who are exposed to it over time. Levels of radon that exceed federal and state guidelines have been found in buildings in Florida. Additional information regarding radon and radon testing may be obtained from your county health department.

LEAD-BASED PAINT
If the building was constructed before 1978, Tenant acknowledges receipt of the federally required lead-based paint disclosure and the pamphlet Protect Your Family From Lead in Your Home.

MOVE-OUT CONDITION
At move-out, Tenant must leave the Home clean, with all trash removed, and in the same condition as at move-in except for normal wear and tear. Tenant should schedule a move-out inspection with the office.

SEVERABILITY
If any provision of this Lease is invalid or unenforceable under applicable law, that provision is ineffective only to the extent of the invalidity, and the remaining provisions of this Lease remain in full force.

ENTIRE AGREEMENT
This Lease, its addenda and the Community Policies are the entire agreement between Landlord and Tenant. No oral promises or representations by Landlord's employees or agents are binding unless written into this Lease or an addendum signed by both parties.

GOVERNING LAW
This Lease is governed by the laws of the State of Florida, including Part II of Chapter 83, Florida Statutes.
