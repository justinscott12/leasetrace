COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Commercial Lease Agreement ("Lease") is entered into on January 15, 2025, between Landlord Properties LLC ("Landlord") and Tenant Retail Inc. ("Tenant") for the premises located at 123 Main Street, Tampa, Pinellas County, Florida 33701.

TERM AND RENT
Section 1.1: The initial term of this Lease shall be three (3) years, commencing on February 1, 2025, and ending on January 31, 2028. The monthly rent shall be $5,000.00, due on the first day of each month.

Section 1.2: Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $10,000.00 upon execution of this Lease. The security deposit shall be held by Landlord and may be applied to any damages, unpaid rent, or other charges owed by Tenant.

LANDLORD OBLIGATIONS
Section 2.1: Landlord shall maintain the structural integrity of the building, including the roof, foundation, and exterior walls. Landlord shall be responsible for repairs to common areas and building systems.

Section 2.2: Landlord shall provide heating and air conditioning systems in good working order. Tenant shall be responsible for maintenance and repairs to HVAC systems after the first year of occupancy.

TENANT OBLIGATIONS
Section 3.1: Tenant shall use the premises solely for retail purposes and shall not use the premises for any illegal activities or purposes that violate local zoning ordinances.

Section 3.2: Tenant shall maintain the premises in good condition and shall not make any alterations or improvements without Landlord's prior written consent.

Section 3.3: Tenant shall be responsible for all utilities, including electricity, water, sewer, and trash removal.

DEFAULT AND TERMINATION
Section 4.1: If Tenant fails to pay rent when due, Landlord may terminate this Lease upon ten (10) days written notice. Tenant shall be liable for all unpaid rent, damages, and attorney's fees.

Section 4.2: If Tenant abandons the premises or fails to occupy the premises for more than thirty (30) consecutive days, Landlord may re-enter and take possession of the premises.

LIABILITY AND INSURANCE
Section 5.1: Tenant shall maintain comprehensive general liability insurance with minimum coverage of $1,000,000.00. Tenant shall indemnify and hold Landlord harmless from any claims arising from Tenant's use of the premises.

Section 5.2: Landlord shall not be liable for any damage to Tenant's personal property, regardless of the cause, including but not limited to theft, fire, water damage, or acts of nature.

ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING
Section 6.1: Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet any portion of the premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which consent may be withheld in Landlord's sole discretion.

HOLDOVER
Section 7.1: If Tenant remains in possession after the expiration of the Lease term without Landlord's written consent, Tenant shall be deemed a holdover tenant and shall pay rent at 150% of the monthly rent amount.

ENTIRE AGREEMENT
Section 8.1: This Lease constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, or agreements. This Lease may only be modified by a written instrument signed by both parties.

GOVERNING LAW
Section 9.1: This Lease shall be governed by the laws of the State of Florida. Any disputes arising under this Lease shall be resolved in the courts of Pinellas County, Florida.
